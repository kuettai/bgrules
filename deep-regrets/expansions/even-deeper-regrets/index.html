🐟 Even Deeper Regrets

An Unfortunate Expansion · Deep Regrets Expansion · 1–5 players · 60–150 min · Tettix Games

📦 What's New

A massive expansion — more new content than the entire base game. Adds a third day option (Inland), new fish for both sea and freshwater, a brand-new Clout mechanic, and the first-ever Pets supply type.

🌊 New Sea Fish

117 new sea fish cards — can be mixed with the base game fish or used as a complete replacement set. Includes new polyps that scale in value based on how many polyps are in play.

🏞️ Inland Area

An entirely new fishing location with its own board:

  • River — 3 shoals, moderate-to-high risk/reward
  • Loch — 1 shoal, highest risk/reward in the game
  • 74 new inland fish + 25 inland dinks

🎣 Equipment

  • 10 new rods
  • 10 new reels

🎒 Supplies & Pets

20 new supply cards including a brand-new type: Pets! Pets provide a persistent benefit that stays active as long as you have them — but they can still be lost at sea.

😰 Regret Cards

7 new regret cards with expanded value range:

  • New 4-value regrets — the heaviest burden yet
  • New −1 value regrets — actually reduce your regret total

💪 Clout Tokens

A brand-new mechanic with wooden Clout tokens drawn from a cloth bag. Clout is earned through inland fishing and scored at end of game.

📋 Solo Mode

New inland solo survey sheet for solo players tackling the inland waters.

🔀 Rule Changes

Inland — The Third Day Option

At the start of each day, players now choose from three options instead of two:

River vs Loch Fishing

When you go Inland, you choose where to fish:

  • River (3 shoals) — more options, spread risk across multiple draws. Higher reward than sea but still manageable.
  • Loch (1 shoal) — single deep draw with the highest risk/reward ratio in the game. Big catches or devastating dinks.
💡 The Loch is tempting but volatile. River fishing lets you hedge across 3 shoals — better for consistent scoring.

Clout Mechanic

Clout is a new resource earned primarily through inland activities:

  • Wooden Clout tokens are kept in a shared cloth bag
  • Certain inland fish and events award Clout tokens
  • Clout is scored at end of game (see Scoring Changes)

Pets (New Supply Type)

Pets are a new category of supply card with unique behaviour:

  • Once acquired, a Pet provides a persistent passive benefit every day
  • Pets stay with you until lost — they can be lost at sea like other supplies
  • You can have multiple Pets active simultaneously
⚠️ Don't get attached — Pets can be lost at sea just like any other supply. Plan accordingly.

Negative Regrets (−1 Value)

Some new regret cards have a −1 value, meaning they actually reduce your total regret. This opens new push-your-luck strategies around deliberately collecting regrets.

Polyp Scaling

New polyp fish have a scaling mechanic: each polyp is worth more for every other polyp in play. Collecting multiple polyps creates exponential value.

🛠️ Setup Changes

Adding the Inland Board

  1. Place the Inland board next to the existing Sea board
  2. Populate the 3 River shoals and 1 Loch shoal with inland fish/dink cards as directed
  3. Place the Clout bag (with wooden tokens inside) within reach of all players

Sea Fish — Mix or Replace

You have two options for the 117 new sea fish:

  • Mix — shuffle expansion sea fish into the base game sea fish for maximum variety
  • Replace — remove all base game sea fish and play with only expansion fish for a fresh experience
💡 First play? Try replacing the base sea fish entirely to experience the new fish without an oversized deck.

New Equipment & Supplies

  • Shuffle the 10 new rods and 10 new reels into their respective market decks
  • Shuffle the 20 new supplies (including Pets) into the supply deck
  • Add the 7 new regret cards to the regret deck

Solo Mode

Solo players take the new inland solo survey sheet in addition to the base game solo sheet.

🏆 Scoring Changes

Clout Scoring

At end of game, each Clout token you've collected is worth points. The exact value depends on how many you've accumulated — more Clout = more points per token.

Expanded Regret Values

The regret deck now includes a wider range:

Regret ValueEffect
−1Reduces your total regret by 1 (new!)

💡 The −1 regrets change the calculus on pushing your luck. Sometimes taking a risk is worth it if you might offset regret.

Polyp Scaling

Each polyp's value increases for every other polyp in play. Collecting a set of polyps can be extremely lucrative — or a wasted effort if you only land one.

⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes
⚠️ Inland is not Port. Going Inland means you're fishing (with risk of dinks), not shopping. You cannot buy supplies or equipment while Inland.
⚠️ Pets can be lost at sea. They're supplies — they follow all supply loss rules. Don't build your strategy around a Pet you might lose.
⚠️ −1 regrets don't cancel other regret cards. They reduce your total regret score, but you still hold the cards. Effects that trigger on "number of regret cards held" still count them.
⚠️ Mixing vs replacing sea fish. If you mix, the sea shoals become much larger and games may feel different. Decide before setup — don't switch mid-game.
⚠️ Polyps need friends. A single polyp is worth very little. Don't draft polyps unless you're committed to collecting several.
⚠️ Loch is 1 shoal, not 3. New players often confuse the Loch with the River. The Loch is a single high-stakes shoal — the River has the 3 shoals.
⚠️ Clout tokens go back in the bag only when specific effects say so. Don't return them automatically at end of day.
