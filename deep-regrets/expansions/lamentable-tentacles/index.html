Lamentable Tentacles

24-card mini-expansion · Tettix Games · 1–5 players

18 Fish Cards · 5 Biggest Regret Cards · 1 Rules Card
What's New

New Fish Cards (18)

  • A mix of real and imagined tentacled fish that shuffle into the existing depth shoals
  • Includes the Great Old One himself — the ultimate horrifying catch
  • New reveal and catch abilities on expansion fish cards

Biggest Regret Cards (5)

  • One per player — tucked under your player mat
  • Each provides an ongoing player-specific ability that changes depending on your current madness level
  • Adds asymmetric powers — every player now has a unique strategic angle
💡 Compatible with solo mode — expansion fish and Biggest Regret cards work with the solo/co-op ichthyologist campaign.
Rule Changes
  • Biggest Regret abilities are ongoing and scale with your madness level — check your card each time your madness changes
  • New expansion fish may introduce additional reveal/catch abilities not present in the base game
  • All other base game rules remain unchanged — fishing, selling, mounting, regrets, and end-game scoring work the same way
Setup Changes
  1. Shuffle all 18 expansion fish cards into their matching depth shoals (Depth I, II, III) with the base game fish
  2. Deal one Biggest Regret card to each player — tuck it under your player mat so the ability is visible
  3. Return unused Biggest Regret cards to the box
  4. Set up the rest of the game as normal
💡 Expansion fish have depth indicators on the card back — sort them by depth before shuffling into the matching shoal.
Scoring Changes
  • No changes to base scoring rules — expansion fish are scored the same way as base game fish (sell value, mount value, eat)
  • Biggest Regret card abilities may indirectly affect scoring by altering what you can catch or how you manage madness, but they don't add a new scoring category
  • The most-regrets penalty still applies: the player with the highest regrets discards their most valuable mounted fish
Gotchas & FAQ
⚠️ Errata — Psychic Interloper: This card is missing the expansion icon on the printed card. It is a Depth II Small fish from this expansion. Shuffle it into the Depth II shoal during setup.
  • Biggest Regret abilities are mandatory — you can't choose to ignore your card's effect when it triggers
  • Expansion fish follow all normal rules for their depth and size — the only new wrinkle is their unique abilities
  • In solo mode, expansion fish are added to the catalog the same way as base game fish
  • Some sources list 18 fish cards; the official Tettix listing confirms 24 total cards (18 fish + 5 Biggest Regret + 1 rules card)
