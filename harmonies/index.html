🏆 Scoring & Winning

Game ends when either: (a) the pouch can't refill the Central board, or (b) a player has ≤2 empty spaces. Finish the round so all players have equal turns.

⚙️ Quick Setup
  1. Place Central board (2–4 player side up). Put all 120 tokens in the pouch.
  2. Draw randomly and fill each of the 5 spaces on the Central board with 3 tokens each.
  3. Shuffle Animal cards → place 5 face-up as a market. Rest = draw pile. Animal cubes nearby.
  4. Each player takes a Personal board. All players agree on Side A or B (use A for first games).
  5. Each player takes a Reminder card matching the chosen board side.
  6. Last player to see a magnificent landscape goes first. Point the Central board arrow at them.
Nature's Spirit cards: Skip these for your first few games. When ready, deal 2 to each player — keep 1, return the other to the box.
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes
You must take ALL 3 tokens from one space. You can't split across spaces or leave tokens behind.

Blue & Yellow can NEVER be stacked.
Water and field tokens always go flat on empty spaces. Nothing goes on top of them, and they can't go on top of anything.

Red tokens can only be 2 high.
A building is red on top of brown, gray, or red. You cannot place red as the 3rd token in a stack.

Animal cubes are permanent.
Once placed, a cube stays even if you later stack tokens that break the original habitat pattern.

Can't stack on top of an animal cube.
A space with a cube on it is locked — no more tokens can go there.

Habitat orientation matters.
The pattern can be rotated but the spatial arrangement must match exactly. Heights must match too.

Max 4 animal cards at a time.
Completed cards (all cubes placed) move to the side and free up a slot.

Isolated mountains = 0 points.
A mountain not adjacent to another mountain scores nothing, regardless of height.

Bigger fields ≠ more points.
Each contiguous group of 2+ yellow tokens = 5 pts, whether it's 2 tokens or 10. Split them up!

Buildings need 3 different adjacent colors.
Only the TOP token of each neighboring stack counts. If a building doesn't have 3+ different colors around it, it scores 0.

Side A: Only your BEST river scores.
If you have multiple rivers, only the longest one counts. Use the shortest path between endpoints.

🌾 Fields — Groups of 2+ adjacent Yellow tokens

Each contiguous group of 2+ yellow tokens = 5 points. Size doesn't matter — a group of 2 scores the same as a group of 8.

Strategy: Make many small separated fields (2 tokens each) rather than one big field.

🏠 Buildings — Red on top of Brown, Gray, or Red

Each building = 5 points IF surrounded by tokens of 3+ different colors (top token only counts). Otherwise 0.

🐾 Animal Cards

Each animal card shows a habitat pattern (specific token arrangement) and has animal cube slots along the right edge. When you match the pattern on your board, move the bottom cube from the card to the matching token.

Key rules:

  • Max 4 cards above your board at once. Completed cards move aside.
  • Pattern can be rotated but must match exactly (colors, heights).
  • Buildings in patterns can have any base (red, brown, or gray underneath).
  • A token can be part of multiple habitats, but only 1 cube per token.
  • Cubes must be taken bottom to top from the card.

Scoring animal cards:

Look at the topmost empty slot (no cube) on the card — that's your score. All cubes still on card = 0 pts. More cubes placed = more points.

Colored stripe on card edge tells you which token color the cube goes on. Avoid taking multiple cards that place cubes on the same color early on — you'll run out of valid spaces.
🦁 Nature's Spirit Cards (Variant)

Add these after a few games for extra depth.

  1. Setup: Shuffle Spirit cards. Deal 2 face-down to each player.
  2. First turn: Choose 1 card, return the other to the box. Place it above your board with a Spirit cube on it.
  3. The Spirit card counts toward your 4-card limit until its cube is placed.
  4. Place the Spirit cube like a normal animal cube when you match its pattern.

Scoring: Spirit cards score only if the cube was placed on your board. Points are calculated at end of game based on your landscapes.

Two types of Spirit cards:

TypeHow It Scores
Landscape CountBonus points for each instance of a specific landscape type you created (e.g., +3 per tree of height 1–2)
Group BonusPoints for groups of connected tokens (e.g., +2 per field of 1–2 tokens, +10 per field of 3+)
Spirit card points are in addition to normal landscape scoring. They can be very lucrative — a well-matched Spirit card can add 15–20+ points.
💡 Strategy Tips
Fields: Aim for many pairs of yellow tokens separated by other colors. Each pair = 5 pts. One big blob of 6 yellow = still just 5 pts.
Mountains: Always cluster them. A lone 3-high mountain = 0 pts. Two adjacent 1-high mountains = 2 pts.
Buildings: Place them at intersections of different terrain types. Plan the surrounding colors before committing.
Animal cards: Don't hoard cards you can't complete. An incomplete card with 0 cubes placed = 0 pts. Even placing 1 cube gets you partial credit.
Watch the token counts: There are only 15 red tokens in the bag. If you see lots of red being taken, pivot your strategy.
Board space is tight! The Personal board has limited hexes. Plan your layout early — running out of space with unplaced tokens is a common trap.
