🐺 Warriors & Druids

Expansion for Here to Slay · Unstable Games · 2021 · Adds 2 new classes

🆕 What's In The Box
  • 2 new Hero classes: Warrior (Wolves) & Druid (Deer)
  • 2 oversized Party Leader cards
  • 2 oversized Monster cards
  • 31 standard-sized cards (Heroes, Items, Magic, Modifiers, Challenges)

With this expansion, the game supports up to 8 players.

⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Shuffle all 31 new standard cards into the Main Deck
  2. Shuffle the 2 new Monsters into the Monster Deck
  3. Add the 2 new Party Leaders to the selection pool
  4. Flip 3 Monsters face-up as normal
Recommended house rule with expansions: Since assembling 6 different classes is easier with 8 classes available, consider requiring a Full Party of all 8 classes for the party win condition — or remove one base class per expansion class added.
📋 Rule Changes
  • Player count: Now supports up to 8 players
  • Full Party win: Still requires 6 different classes for a Full Party (official rules) — but with 8 classes available, this becomes easier
  • All other rules remain unchanged — same AP system, same challenge mechanics
💡 Strategy Notes
  • Piercing Howl synergy: Load Items onto your Heroes to build massive roll bonuses
  • Noble Shaman is top-tier defensive: Reducing rolls by 1 every turn disrupts Hero abilities and Monster attacks across the table
  • Warriors tend to have aggressive, combat-oriented abilities
  • Druids reward low rolls — pair with negative Modifiers on yourself for guaranteed activations
