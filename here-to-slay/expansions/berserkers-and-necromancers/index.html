🦁 Berserkers & Necromancers

Expansion for Here to Slay · Unstable Games · 2021 · Adds 2 new classes

🆕 What's In The Box
  • 2 new Hero classes: Berserker (Lions) & Necromancer (Dogs)
  • 2 oversized Party Leader cards
  • 2 oversized Monster cards
  • 31 standard-sized cards (Heroes, Items, Magic, Modifiers, Challenges)

With this expansion, the game supports up to 8 players (or 10 if combined with Warriors & Druids).

👑 New Party Leaders
NameClassAbility
The Raging ManticoreBerserkerEach time you Slay a Monster, draw 2 cards
The Gnawing DreadNecromancerYou may spend 2 AP to search the discard pile for any card and add it to your hand
The Gnawing Dread is extremely versatile — recover destroyed Heroes, key Magic cards, or crucial Modifiers from the discard pile.
The Raging Manticore only triggers on successful Monster slays, making it situational. Plan your strategy accordingly.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Shuffle all 31 new standard cards into the Main Deck
  2. Shuffle the 2 new Monsters into the Monster Deck
  3. Add the 2 new Party Leaders to the selection pool
  4. Flip 3 Monsters face-up as normal
Combining with Warriors & Druids: With 10 classes available, a Full Party of 6 is very achievable. Consider requiring 8 different classes for the party win condition as a house rule.
📋 Rule Changes
  • Player count: Supports up to 8 players (alone) or 10 combined with Warriors & Druids
  • Full Party win: Officially still 6 different classes
  • All other rules remain unchanged
💡 Strategy Notes
  • Berserkers excel at explosive turns — save Modifiers for their high-threshold abilities
  • Necromancers recycle the discard pile — great for late-game comebacks
  • Gnawing Dread + discard pile awareness: Track what's been discarded to maximize value
  • Raging Manticore pairs well with Monster-heavy strategies — the card draw snowballs quickly after your first slay
  • Berserkers are fragile early: Their high roll requirements mean frequent failed activations — build support before going aggressive
