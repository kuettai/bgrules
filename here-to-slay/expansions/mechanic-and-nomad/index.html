Kickstarter Exclusive Expansion for Here to Slay · Unstable Games · 2020

🆕 What's In The Box
  • 2 new Hero classes: Mechanic & Nomad (Kickstarter Exclusive)
  • New Hero cards for each class with unique abilities
  • Additional Item, Magic, Modifier, and Challenge cards
  • New Party Leader cards (multi-class hybrid leaders)
Kickstarter Exclusive: This expansion was only available to backers of the 2020 Here to Slay Kickstarter campaign. It is not available at retail.

⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Shuffle all new standard-sized cards into the Main Deck
  2. Add new Party Leaders to the selection pool
  3. If adding new Monsters, shuffle them into the Monster Deck
  4. All other setup follows base game rules
Combining with retail expansions: KSE cards mix seamlessly with Warriors & Druids and Berserkers & Necromancers. Consider balancing the Full Party win requirement when playing with many expansions.
📋 Rule Changes
  • Multi-class Party Leaders count for both classes toward Full Party requirements
  • The Unstable Unicorn has no class — it does not count toward any class requirement for Full Party
  • All other rules unchanged — same AP system, same challenge mechanics
💡 Strategy Notes
  • Multi-class leaders give a massive head start: You only need 4 more unique classes instead of 5 for Full Party
  • The Unstable Unicorn trades class advantage for a powerful unique ability — consider if you plan to win via Monster slaying
  • Mechanic heroes synergize with Items — pair with Warriors & Druids' Piercing Howl for maximum stacking
  • Nomad heroes offer flexibility — their class-shifting abilities let you adapt your strategy mid-game
