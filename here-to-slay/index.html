🏆 Win Conditions

Be the first player to achieve either:

  • Slay 3 Monsters — attack and defeat monsters from the central row
  • Full Party — end your turn with at least one Hero of each of the 6 classes in your party (including your Party Leader's class)
Instant win: The moment either condition is met, that player wins immediately.
⚙️ Setup
  1. Separate Party Leaders (oversized, light backs), Monster cards (oversized), and Main Deck
  2. Shuffle the Monster deck and flip 3 face-up in the centre
  3. Roll dice to determine who picks their Party Leader first — each player selects one and places it face-up in front of them
  4. Shuffle the Main Deck (Hero, Item, Magic, Modifier, Challenge cards)
  5. Deal 5 cards to each player — this is your starting hand
  6. Give each player a reference card
  7. The player who picked their Party Leader last goes first
The Shadow Claw cannot be used in 2-player games.
🔄 Turn Structure

Each turn you have 3 Action Points (AP). Spend them in any order:

CostAction
1 APDraw a card from the Main Deck
1 APPlay a Hero, Item, or Magic card from hand
1 APUse a Hero's ability (max once per Hero per turn, requires dice roll)
2 APAttack a Monster (must meet party requirements)
3 APDiscard entire hand and draw 5 new cards (ends turn)

You may repeat actions (e.g., draw twice) and end your turn early. AP does not carry over.

Even if a Hero's roll fails, the AP is still spent. Plan carefully!
🃏 Card Types

Party Leader (Oversized)

  • Placed at game start — counts as a Hero of its class
  • Has a passive/ongoing ability
  • Cannot be destroyed, stolen, sacrificed, or discarded

Hero Cards

  • Played to your party (1 AP) — opponents may Challenge
  • Each has a unique ability requiring a dice roll to activate (1 AP)
  • Belong to one of 6 classes: Fighter, Guardian, Ranger, Thief, Bard, Wizard

Item Cards

  • Attach to a Hero in any player's party (slide under the Hero)
  • Max 1 item per Hero (unless card says otherwise)
  • Positive items → your Heroes; Cursed items → opponents' Heroes
  • Items cannot be equipped to Party Leaders
  • If the Hero is destroyed/stolen, the item goes with it

Magic Cards

  • Powerful one-time effects — immediately discarded after use
  • Can be Challenged by opponents

Modifier Cards

  • Adjust any dice roll by +/− values shown on the card
  • Can be played by any player at any time on anyone's roll
  • Multiple modifiers can stack
  • Does NOT cost an AP — played from hand freely

Challenge Cards

  • Played immediately after someone plays a Hero, Item, or Magic card
  • Both players roll 2d6 — highest total wins (ties go to challenger)
  • Modifiers may be applied to challenge rolls
  • If challenger wins → target card is discarded (AP still spent)
  • If target wins → card takes effect normally
  • Cannot challenge a Challenge or Modifier card
  • Only one Challenge per card played

Monster Cards (Oversized)

  • 3 face-up in centre at all times (refill from deck when slain)
  • Each has specific party requirements to attack (class combos or Hero count)
  • Roll 2d6 to resolve — success/fail/neutral thresholds on card
  • Slain monsters give a permanent ability and count toward win
  • Failed attacks may require sacrificing a Hero

🐉 Attacking Monsters
  1. Check requirements: Your party must meet the class/hero requirements listed on the Monster card. Party Leaders count for their class requirement but NOT as a generic "Hero".
  2. Spend 2 AP and declare your attack
  3. Roll 2d6 — opponents may play Modifier cards
  4. Resolve outcome:
    • Success (≥ threshold): Monster is slain! Place it beside your party. Gain its ongoing ability. Flip a new Monster from the deck.
    • Failure (≤ threshold): Pay the penalty (usually sacrifice a Hero)
    • Neutral (middle range): Nothing happens
Opponents can play Modifiers to sabotage your roll — attack when they have few cards in hand!

💡 Strategy Tips
  • Be the grey man: Don't rush ahead — players with many Heroes become targets
  • Hold Modifiers for defence: Keep some in hand to protect important rolls
  • Attack monsters when opponents are low on cards — fewer negative Modifiers to play
  • Watch opponent's classes: Someone at 4–5 different classes could win next turn
  • Same-class Heroes = stronger abilities, but harder to slay Monsters
  • Focus on one win condition but be ready to pivot
❓ FAQ & Common Mistakes

Is there a hand limit?

No official hand limit. Popular house rule: 7-card max at end of turn.

Can you Challenge a Challenge?

No. Challenges can only target Hero, Item, or Magic cards being played.

Can you roll for a Hero's ability more than once?

No. Each Hero may only attempt its ability once per turn, regardless of success/failure.

How many Items can a Hero have?

One unless the card has a double-diamond symbol indicating otherwise. Items cannot be swapped out.

Can Items be equipped to Party Leaders?

No. Items are only equippable to Hero cards.

Do Party Leaders count as "Heroes" for Monster requirements?

Party Leaders count for their class requirement but do NOT count as a generic "Hero" requirement.

What happens to Items when a Hero is destroyed/stolen?

If a Hero is stolen, items go with it. If destroyed, items are discarded.

Ties in Challenges?

Challenger wins all ties.
