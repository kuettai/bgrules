🌊 Railroad Tiles: Lakes Expansion

Expansion · 1–4 players · ~30 min · Horrible Guild

📦 What's New
  • Lake tiles — new tiles with lake edges that form bodies of water in your landscape.
  • Harbor tokens — created automatically when a road or rail route touches the lake side of a tile.
  • Ferry meeples — cute new pawns placed on lake pin points; work like trains/cars but with unique placement rules.
  • Lake connectivity — all routes connected to the same lake via Harbors count as connected to each other. Lakes act as big Stations!
  • New objective tiles — including Fishermen's Island (reachable by 3+ Traveler pawns) and Hydroelectric Plant (closed lake, no open sides).
  • Replacement circular discs — some base game discs are swapped for ones showing Ferry symbols.
💡 Widely considered one of the best Railroad Tiles expansions — big satisfying scores with no penalties or downsides. If you only get one expansion, this is a top pick.
🔀 Rule Changes

Harbors

When you place a tile so that a road or rail route leads into the lake edge of a tile, a Harbor is created at that connection point. You don't spend anything — it happens automatically.

Lake Connectivity (The Big One)

All routes that connect to the same lake via Harbors are treated as connected to each other. This means separate road networks and rail networks can effectively merge through a shared lake.

💡 This is the expansion's superpower. A single well-placed lake can unify multiple disconnected networks, massively boosting meeple scoring.

Ferry Placement

At the end of each round, after resolving your normal Placement tokens:

  1. Check each of your lakes.
  2. If a lake has more Harbors than Ferries, place 1 Ferry pawn for free on any empty Ferry pin point in that lake.
  3. Repeat until Ferries equal Harbors in that lake (1 free Ferry per excess Harbor).
💡 Ferries are placed for free — they don't replace one of your 2 normal Placement token placements. They're a bonus on top.

⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Set up the base game as normal.
  2. Replace the specified circular discs with the Lakes expansion discs (showing Ferry symbols).
  3. Add the lake tiles to the expansion bag — during display refresh, replace 1–2 base tiles per column with lake tiles drawn from this bag.
  4. Place Ferry meeples in the general supply.
  5. Optionally shuffle in the new objective tiles (Fishermen's Island, Hydroelectric Plant, etc.).
💡 Like all Railroad Tiles expansions, only one expansion is used per game. The total number of rounds (8) stays the same.
🏆 Scoring Changes

New Scoring

  • Ferries — 1 pt + 1 pt per other Ferry in the same lake (max 3 pts each). Placed for free, so they're pure bonus points.
  • Harbor connectivity — routes sharing a lake are connected, so existing train/car/passenger meeples in those networks score higher due to larger connected networks.

Unchanged

  • Trains, cars, and passengers still score 1 + (same-type in network), max 5 pts each.
  • Rectangle scoring, city scoring, and open-end penalties all remain the same.
💡 The real scoring power isn't just the Ferries themselves — it's that lake connectivity inflates your existing train/car/passenger scores by merging networks.
⚠️ Gotchas
⚠️ Ferries cap at 3 pts, not 5. Unlike trains/cars/passengers (max 5 VP each), Ferry meeples max out at 3 Prestige points. They compensate by being free to place.
⚠️ Ferries are bonus placements. They don't consume one of your 2 Placement token slots. You place them after your normal meeple placements each round.
⚠️ Harbor creation is automatic. Any route touching a lake edge creates a Harbor — you can't opt out. Plan your tile placement accordingly.
⚠️ Lake connectivity merges networks. Two separate road networks both ending in Harbors on the same lake are now one network for scoring. This can dramatically change car/train/passenger point totals mid-game.
⚠️ Closed lakes matter for objectives. The Hydroelectric Plant objective requires a lake with no open sides. Make sure every edge of every tile forming that lake connects to another lake tile or a Harbor route.
⚠️ One expansion per game. You cannot combine Lakes with Canals, Forest, or any other expansion in the same game.
