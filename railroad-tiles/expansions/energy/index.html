⚡ Energy Expansion

Railroad Tiles Expansion · 1–4 players · 30 min · Horrible Guild · Ages 8+

📦 What's New

🌀 Wind Turbine Meeples

New wooden meeples representing wind turbines. They have their own pin points on tiles and can be placed by spending any Placement token as a wild card (instead of placing the corresponding standard pawn).

⚡ Energy Cables — Diagonal Connectivity

A brand-new route type that connects tiles at their corners instead of across the middle of edges (like railways and highways). This is the only expansion that introduces diagonal connectivity.

💡 A single cable at the right corner can connect up to 3 tiles to the power grid in one go — plan your corners carefully!

🔀 Rule Changes

Placing Wind Turbines

  • Wind Turbines have dedicated pin points printed on certain tiles
  • To place a Wind Turbine, spend any Placement token as a wild card — you forfeit placing the corresponding standard pawn that turn
  • Wind Turbines score 0 points when placed — their value comes at end-game scoring

Energy Cables — The Diagonal Route

  • Energy Cables are a new route type alongside railways and highways
  • They run across tile corners, not the middle of tile edges
  • Use cables to connect Wind Turbines to Town tiles, forming a power grid
  • A corner is shared by up to 4 tiles — one cable at a corner can reach multiple tiles
⚠️ Energy Cables do NOT connect via edges like railways/highways. They only connect diagonally through corners. Don't mix up the two connection types.

Objective Tiles

New objective tiles are added to the game. Each has a unique condition tied to the energy network (e.g., Solar Farm needs cables on all 4 corners, Casino needs 2+ electrified towns adjacent).

⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Add the Energy Expansion tiles to the tile supply (some replace base game tiles — check the rulebook for which ones)
  2. Replace the specified circular discs from the base game with the expansion's replacements
  3. Place the Wind Turbine meeples in a shared supply
  4. Shuffle the new Objective tiles (Laboratory, Solar Farm, Casino) into the objective pool
  5. Set up all other base game components as normal
💡 Keep the Wind Turbine meeples separate from other pawns — they're placed via wild card, not their own dedicated token.
🏆 Scoring Changes

Town Tile Scoring (End of Game)

Each of your Town tiles scores 1 point per 2 Wind Turbines it is connected to via the energy cable network.

Objective Tile Scoring

⚠️ Gotchas
⚠️ Corners, not edges. Energy Cables connect diagonally through tile corners. Railways and highways connect through the middle of tile edges. These are completely separate networks.
⚠️ Wind Turbines cost a pawn placement. You must spend a Placement token as a wild card to place a turbine — you lose that pawn placement for the turn.
⚠️ Turbines score 0 when placed. All turbine scoring happens at end of game through connected Town tiles. Don't expect immediate points.
⚠️ 1 point per 2 turbines. A Town connected to only 1 turbine scores nothing. You need pairs — plan your cable network to connect at least 2 turbines per town.
⚠️ Replace base game components. Some base tiles and circular discs are swapped out — don't just add everything on top. Check the rulebook for which pieces to remove.
⚠️ Solar Farm needs ALL 4 corners. Not 3, not "most" — all four corners must have Energy Cable connections for the Solar Farm objective to score.
