🌍 Railroad Tiles: World Expansion

Expansion · 1–4 players · ~30 min · Horrible Guild · Mixes with base game + any other expansion

📦 What's New

The World Expansion is unique among Railroad Tiles expansions — it can be combined with the base game and any other thematic expansion (whereas other expansions are mutually exclusive). It adds two things:

  • New Objective tiles — additional goals that work identically to the base game objectives, expanding the pool you draw from during setup
  • Special Pawns — upgraded versions of the 3 standard meeple types (Cars, Trains, Travelers) with unique placement rules and bonus scoring conditions
More variety, no new mechanics. This expansion is ideal if you enjoy the base game's core loop and just want extra decision points without learning new subsystems.
🔀 Rule Changes

Special Pawns

Each Special Pawn is tied to one of the three base types and follows that type's placement rules, plus its own twist:

Special PawnBase TypeSpecial Ability
🚌 BusCarSpecial Car pawn with its own placement/scoring condition
🏎️ Sports CarCarWorth 2 extra VP when matching its specific condition
🔧 MechanicTravelerCan be placed on a Train pinpoint (in addition to Traveler pinpoints); +1 VP if placed on a Train pinpoint

Placement prerequisite: To place any Special Pawn, you must already have at least 2 regular pawns of the same type placed on your network. They don't need to be connected to each other or to the Special Pawn.

Timing: You place a Special Pawn when the matching Placement token type comes up on the Station board — same trigger as a regular pawn of that type.

2-pawn minimum is mandatory. You cannot place a Special Car pawn (Bus, Sports Car) unless you already have at least 2 Car pawns on your board. Same rule applies for Train and Traveler specials.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Shuffle new Objectives in — add the World Expansion objective tiles to the base game's objective pool before selecting your 3 random objectives
  2. Give each player their Special Pawns — each player receives the set of Special Pawns to keep near their play area
  3. All other setup is identical to the base game (or base game + whichever thematic expansion you're using)
Stacks with other expansions. Unlike thematic expansions (Forest, Lakes, Monuments, etc.) which are mutually exclusive, the World Expansion layers on top of any single thematic expansion — or just the base game alone.
🏆 Scoring Changes

All base game scoring remains unchanged. The World Expansion adds:

  • New Objectives — each is worth 5 VP at end of game if its condition is met (same as base objectives). Example: the Observatory wants to be in a row or column with at least 6 tiles.
  • Special Pawn bonuses — each Special Pawn scores like a regular pawn of its type (1 VP + 1 per same-type pawn in the same network, max 5 VP), plus its individual bonus VP when its condition is met
Bonus VP is incremental. A Special Pawn's extra 1–2 VP on top of normal network scoring can matter in a game where final scores are typically 50–60 VP. Every point counts.
⚠️ Gotchas

Don't forget the 2-pawn prerequisite.
The most common mistake — you cannot place a Special Pawn until you have 2 regular pawns of that type already on your board. Plan ahead so you don't miss the window.

Special Pawns still need the right Placement token.
Having 2 Cars on your board doesn't let you place a Sports Car whenever you want. You still need a Car-type Placement token to be active on the conveyer belt.

New Objectives mix into the full pool.
You might draw all base objectives, all World objectives, or a mix. There's no guarantee you'll see the new ones in any given game.

Special Pawns are optional.
When the right token comes up and you meet the prerequisite, you choose whether to place a Special Pawn or a regular one. Sometimes the regular pawn is the better play if the Special's condition doesn't apply.

Network connection still matters for scoring. The Special Pawn's 2-pawn prerequisite doesn't require connection, but its scoring still counts same-type pawns in the same connected network. Place strategically for maximum points.
