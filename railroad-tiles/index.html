⭐ Cleanup

1. Refresh: Reveal the next Placement Token. If it has a 🚩 flag, every player places one Objective tile into their tableau. Refill empty columns with tiles from the bag.

2. Draft: In turn order, each player picks a column of tiles (2, 3, or 4 tiles). The column you pick determines your turn order next round — fewer tiles = earlier pick next round.

3. Place Tiles: Immediately add all drafted tiles to your tableau. Each tile must be adjacent (side-to-side) to at least one existing tile. Matching edges required:

  • 🛤️ Railroad edge → Railroad edge
  • 🛣️ Highway edge → Highway edge
  • 🌿 Empty edge → Empty edge
⚠️ You cannot rotate/flip a tile to break edge-matching rules. All four sides of every tile must match their neighbors.

4. Place Pawns: Check all active Placement Tokens. For each token, gain the matching pawn and place it on a Pinpoint in your tableau:

  • 🚗 Car (blue) — placed on Highway Pinpoints
  • 🚂 Train (green) — placed on Railroad Pinpoints
  • 🧳 Traveler (red) — placed on either Highway or Railroad Pinpoints

Score immediately when placing: 1 VP + 1 VP per same-type pawn already connected in that network (max 5 VP per pawn).

💡 Travelers use both road and rail networks — they score off every connected traveler regardless of whether the link is highway or railroad. Cars and trains only count their own network type.

5. Cleanup: The undrafted column stays; add a ⭐ Star token to it. Move turn-order markers based on this round's draft picks.

Placement Token Stack (Conveyer Belt):

Each round reveals one circular Placement Token. All revealed tokens are active — so by round 3 you place 3 pawns per round. Tokens show 🚗, 🚂, or 🧳 (some show a choice of two).

⭐ Star Tokens: Spend during Pawn Placement to change one pawn into any other type. Unspent stars = 1 VP each at end of game.

🏆 Scoring & End Game

The game ends after 8 rounds. Final scoring:

📐 +1 VP/tile Largest Rectangle
🏙️ +5 VP each Cities (3+ Towns)
🚶 During game Pawn Placements
⭐ +1 VP each Unspent Stars
🚫 −1 VP each Open Ends (after 5 free)
🎯 +5 VP each Completed Objectives

📐 Largest Rectangle: Find the biggest fully-filled rectangle of tiles in your tableau. Count every tile inside it — that's your bonus. This is often the single biggest scoring category (commonly 15–25+ VP).

💡 The rectangle must be completely filled — no gaps. Prioritize building a compact, dense grid rather than sprawling outward.

🏙️ Cities: A City = 3 or more adjacent Town tiles (orthogonal only, no diagonals). Each distinct city scores 5 VP regardless of size. A 3-tile city and a 7-tile city both score 5 VP — so aim for multiple small cities!

🚫 Open Ends: Any tile edge with an unconnected Railroad or Highway counts as an opening. Your first 5 openings are free; each additional opening costs −1 VP.

⚠️ Open ends add up fast in the late game. Plan your final rounds to close off dangling connections.

Typical scores: 25–60 VP range across 4 plays. Highest scores come from large rectangles + chained pawn networks.

⚙️ Quick Setup
  1. Shuffle all Route Tiles into the cloth bag.
  2. Place the Station Board in the center of the table.
  3. Shuffle all Placement Tokens face-down. Create a face-down pile on the Clock space of the Station Board.
  4. Sort pawns by type: 🚗 Cars (blue), 🚂 Trains (green), 🧳 Travelers (red). Place nearby with Prestige tokens (1/3/10/20).
  5. Place ⭐ Star tokens in a supply.
  6. Each player takes a Player Pawn and places it in the Turn Order area.
  7. Draw tiles from the bag to fill the Station Board columns (2/3/4 tiles per column depending on player count).
  8. Round 1 draft: Each player drafts one starting tile (or deal 1 random tile each for faster setup).

With Objectives (Advanced Mode):

  1. Choose 3 of the 7 Objective tile types.
  2. Give each player the same set of 3 Objective tiles (set aside, not in tableau yet).
  3. Objectives enter play when a Placement Token with a 🚩 flag is revealed.
💡 For your first game, skip Objectives. Add them in your second play — they're easy to learn and add meaningful decisions.
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes
  • All 4 edges must match. Not just the side you're connecting — check every adjacent tile edge.
  • Pawns score immediately when placed, not at end of game. Take Prestige tokens right away.
  • Travelers use both networks. Cars only count highway connections; trains only count rail. But travelers count connections through both road and rail — making them potentially more valuable.
  • Max 5 VP per pawn. Even if your network has 6+ same-type pawns, each new pawn scores at most 5 VP (1 base + 4 connected).
  • Cities need exactly 3+ adjacent Towns. Diagonals don't count. Two adjacent towns = no city. A city of any size = 5 VP (not scaled by size).
  • Open ends: first 5 are free. Only the 6th, 7th, 8th… cost −1 VP each. Don't panic about a few loose ends early on.
  • Star tokens can change pawn type. Spend 1 star during Pawn Placement to swap one pawn for any other type. Don't forget this!
  • Undrafted column carries over with a Star token added. It can become very attractive next round.
  • 2-player token: In 2P games, flip the 2-Player Token each round to determine which leftover column to discard. Easy to forget!
  • Placement Tokens accumulate. By round 3 you're placing 3 pawns per round. Don't skip earlier tokens — all revealed tokens are active every round.
⚠️ The most common mistake: forgetting that ALL active Placement Tokens grant pawns each round, not just the newly revealed one.
🎯 Goal Tiles (Advanced Mode)

The game includes 7 types of Objective tiles (4 copies each). Choose 3 types per game and give each player the same set of 3.

Objectives are placed into your tableau when a Placement Token with a 🚩 flag is revealed. They follow normal tile placement rules and score 5 VP each if completed.

Objective tiles act as normal tiles — they have edges (Railroad/Highway/Empty) and fit into your grid. But each has a unique scoring condition based on what surrounds it or how your tableau is built.

💡 Plan ahead for where you'll place Objectives. Since you know which 3 you have from the start, you can shape your tableau to accommodate them before the flag appears.
⚠️ Some Objectives (like the Stadium) require specific Pinpoint types on surrounding tiles. If the right tiles never come out, you may not complete it — don't over-commit to a single Objective.

Network rules by pawn type:

  • 🚗 Cars: Only count other cars connected via continuous Highway. A station/exchange breaks the highway network.
  • 🚂 Trains: Only count other trains connected via continuous Railroad. A station/exchange breaks the rail network.
  • 🧳 Travelers: Count other travelers connected via any combination of Highway and Railroad. Stations/exchanges do NOT break traveler networks.
💡 Travelers are the most flexible pawn type. Building one long mixed network and loading it with travelers can be very efficient — but you're at the mercy of which Placement Tokens come out.

Example: You place a 🚗 Car on a highway that already has 2 other 🚗 Cars connected. You score 3 VP (1 base + 2 for existing cars). Take 3 Prestige tokens immediately.

🧑 Solo Mode

Solo mode uses the same core rules with these changes:

  • Set up the Station Board as normal with tile columns.
  • Each round, pick one column of tiles. Discard the rest.
  • No turn-order considerations — just optimize your own tableau.
  • The game includes 15 solo missions with 1–3 star difficulty ratings.
  • Missions focus on specific goals: town tiles, certain Pinpoint colors, creating multiple cities, hitting score thresholds, etc.
💡 Solo target scores are typically 50–60+ VP. Focus on building a tight rectangle and chaining pawn networks efficiently.
🧱 Tile Types Reference

Tiles come in two body types:

  • Field tiles — green/open landscape. No special scoring.
  • Town tiles — blue buildings. Group 3+ adjacent towns (orthogonal) to form a City (5 VP).

Each tile has 4 edges, each one of:

  • 🛤️ Railroad — must connect to another Railroad edge
  • 🛣️ Highway — must connect to another Highway edge
  • 🌿 Empty — must connect to another Empty edge

Some tiles have Pinpoints (small GPS-like icons) in blue (Car), green (Train), or red (Traveler). These are the spots where you place pawns.

Some tiles have Stations/Exchanges — where a road meets a rail. These break Car and Train networks but NOT Traveler networks.
