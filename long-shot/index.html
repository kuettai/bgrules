🏆 Winning Condition / Scoring / Objective

The game ends when 3 horses cross the finish line. Finish the current turn, then score. Most money wins.

⚙️ Quick Setup
  1. Place racetrack board in center. Line up all 8 horses behind the starting line.
  2. Choose a horse card set (Apple Core 🍎 or Gate 🚪, or mix) — lay all 8 cards face-up next to the track.
  3. Give each player: 1 player board + 1 dry-erase marker.
  4. Shuffle starting cards, deal 1 each. Mark the 4 concession numbers and 2 free bets shown on your card.
  5. Everyone writes $12 as starting cash.
  6. Whoever most recently made a bet goes first and takes the dice.

⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes

Concession moves can't finish a horse.
A "move ahead" concession bonus can never push a horse across the finish line. Stop it just before. You also can't move a horse backward past the finish line.

No betting past the no-bet line — unless you have the helmet.
Once a horse crosses the red line, you can only bet on it if you marked its helmet.

3rd horse finishes mid-turn? Keep going.
Finish the current turn so all players get their action. But no more horses can cross the finish line — they stop just before it.

Can't buy owned or finished horses.
If someone already owns it or it crossed the finish line, it's off the market.

Bets on horses that didn't reach the no-bet line = $0.
That money is gone. Horses past the line but not placing get your bet back (×1).

Diagonals don't count on the concession grid.
Only completed rows and columns earn bonuses.

Secondary movement resolves low → high.
Always resolve horse #1 first, then #2, etc. This order can matter.

Secondary movement CAN finish a horse.
Card X's (secondary movement) can send a horse across the line. Only concession bonuses can't.

Actions resolve in clockwise order from the roller.
Turn order matters — especially near the end of the race.

Turn Structure (detailed)
  1. Roll dice — active player rolls the d8 (horse) + d6 (movement, faces: 1-2-2-2-3-3).
  2. Move primary horse — the horse matching the d8 moves the number of spaces on the d6.
  3. Secondary movement — check that horse's card for X marks. Each marked horse moves exactly 1 space. Resolve low → high.
  4. All players take 1 action — in clockwise order from the active player. The action is tied to the horse number rolled.
  5. Pass dice — next player clockwise becomes active.
Actions (detailed)

Your action always relates to the horse number rolled on the d8.

ActionWhat You Do
ConcessionMark a matching number on your 4×4 grid. Completing a row or column earns a bonus (money, free bet, move horses, free helmet/jersey/horse).
HelmetMark the helmet for the rolled horse. Lets you bet on it even after it passes the no-bet line.
JerseyMark the jersey for the rolled horse AND add an X to that horse's card (more secondary movement).
BetBet up to $3 on the rolled horse. Subtract from your cash. Can increase existing bets.
Buy HorsePay the price to own the rolled horse. Gain its special ability + prize money if it places top 3. Can't buy owned or finished horses.
Wild Horseshoes (×3): Spend one to use ANY horse number instead of what was rolled. You can skip your action to recover a spent horseshoe.
Concession Bonuses (detailed)

When you complete a row or column in your 4×4 concession grid, pick one unclaimed bonus:

  • Gain money ($5 or $7)
  • Free $3 bet on any horse (doesn't cost cash)
  • Get any horse's helmet or jersey for free
  • Buy any available horse for free
  • Move one or two horses forward or backward a number of spaces
Important: A concession "move ahead" bonus can NEVER send a horse across the finish line. Move it to just before the line at most. You also can't move a horse backward past the finish line.

If you complete multiple rows/columns at once, you claim multiple bonuses. Diagonals don't count.
