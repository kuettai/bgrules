🌱 Plots & Irrigation

Placing Plots

A new plot must be placed either:

  • Adjacent to the central Pond tile, OR
  • Adjacent to at least 2 existing plots

Each plot grows one bamboo shoot of its color (green, yellow, or pink).

Irrigation Rules

A plot is irrigated if any of these are true:

  • Adjacent to the Pond tile (automatic)
  • Has at least one irrigation channel on its edge
  • Has the Watershed improvement

Irrigation channels form a network starting from the Pond's corners. When a plot is irrigated for the first time, 1 bamboo section grows immediately.

Free placement: Stored irrigation channels can be placed at any time during your turn without spending an action.
Bamboo only grows on irrigated plots. The Gardener landing on a non-irrigated plot does nothing.
👨‍🌾 Gardener & 🐼 Panda Movement

Movement Rules (both)

  • Move in a straight line, any number of plots
  • Cannot jump over empty spaces
  • Must move at least 1 plot

Gardener

When the Gardener stops: grow 1 bamboo on that plot AND on all adjacent irrigated plots of the same color.

Panda

When the Panda stops: eat 1 bamboo section from that plot. Store it on your player board for Panda objectives.

Max bamboo height: 4 sections per plot. No growth beyond that.
🔧 Improvements

Obtained via ☁️ Clouds weather. Place on a plot with no bamboo currently growing (just placed, not irrigated, or panda ate everything). One improvement per plot, permanent.

ImprovementEffect
🚧 EnclosurePanda cannot eat bamboo here (can still move through/stop)
🌿 FertilizerGrows 2 bamboo sections instead of 1 each time (still max 4)
💧 WatershedPlot is automatically irrigated (cannot start irrigation networks)
Built-in improvements (printed on tiles) count — that plot cannot receive another chip.
🎯 Objective Cards

Three categories, each with its own draw pile:

🟩 Plot Objectives

Show a pattern of 3–4 adjacent plots of specific colors. All plots in the pattern must be irrigated to count.

🟨 Gardener Objectives

Require bamboo shoots at specific heights:

  • A single 4-high shoot with a specific improvement (or no improvement)
  • Multiple 3-high shoots of a color (no improvement constraint)

🟪 Panda Objectives

Require 2–3 bamboo sections of specific colors on your player board. Sections are returned to the reserve when completed.

Objectives are permanent: Once completed, they stay scored even if the board state changes later.
Hand limit: Max 5 objective cards. You must complete at least one before drawing again if at 5.
🏆 End Game & Scoring

Triggering the End

PlayersObjectives to Trigger
2 players9 completed objectives
3 players8 completed objectives
4 players7 completed objectives

The triggering player takes the Emperor card (worth 2 points) and finishes their turn. All other players get one final turn.

Scoring

Total the points on all completed objective cards + Emperor card (if applicable).

Tiebreaker: Most points from Panda objectives. Still tied? Shared victory.

You can complete multiple objectives on the turn you trigger the end.
⚙️ Setup
  1. Place the Pond tile in the center of the table
  2. Place the Gardener and Panda on the Pond tile
  3. Shuffle remaining plot tiles face-down as a draw pile
  4. Place irrigation channels and improvements (sorted by type) as reserves
  5. Set the Emperor card aside
  6. Shuffle objective cards into 3 piles by category (Plot / Gardener / Panda)
  7. Each player takes: 1 player board, 2 action chips, and 1 card from each objective pile
  8. Tallest player starts — play clockwise
📦 Components
  • 28 hex plot tiles (11 green, 9 yellow, 7 pink + 1 Pond)
  • 90 bamboo sections (36 green, 30 yellow, 24 pink)
  • 20 irrigation channels
  • 9 improvement chips (3 of each type)
  • 46 objective cards (Plot / Gardener / Panda)
  • 4 player boards + 8 action chips
  • 1 weather die
  • 1 Gardener figurine + 1 Panda figurine
  • 1 Emperor card (2 VP)
❓ FAQ & Gotchas
Gardener grows bamboo only on irrigated plots. If he stops on a non-irrigated plot, nothing happens there (but adjacent irrigated same-color plots still grow).
Panda on an empty plot: If there's no bamboo, the Panda eats nothing.
Enclosure doesn't block the Panda from stopping — it only prevents eating.

Common Questions

  • Can the Gardener/Panda stay on the Pond? — Yes, they start there. The Pond is not a colored plot so no bamboo grows.
  • Can I place irrigation on the Pond's edges? — No. Irrigation starts from the Pond's corners, not its edges.
  • Does Fertilizer work with Rain weather? — Yes! Rain on a Fertilizer plot adds 2 sections (up to max 4).
  • Can I complete objectives on other players' turns? — No, only on your own turn.
  • What if the plot draw pile runs out? — The Plot action is no longer available.
  • Storm Panda placement: The Panda moves to ANY plot (not in a straight line) and eats 1 bamboo.
