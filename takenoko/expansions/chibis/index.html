🐼 Takenoko: Chibis

Antoine Bauza & Corentin Lebrat · 2015 · Expansion for Takenoko

🆕 What's New
  • Miss Panda figurine — a new character
  • 9 Panda Baby tokens (3 green, 3 yellow, 3 pink) with bonus backs
  • 6 new special plot tiles (marked with ⭐ symbol)
  • 18 new objective cards (6 Plot, 6 Gardener, 6 Panda)
  • 17 additional bamboo sections
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Shuffle the 6 new plots (⭐) into the base game plot pile
  2. Shuffle new objective cards into their respective category piles
  3. Place Panda Baby tokens and Miss Panda figurine near the play area
Miss Panda does NOT start on the board. She appears when the first ⭐ plot is placed.
🐼 Miss Panda

Entering Play

When a player places a ⭐ plot tile, Miss Panda is immediately placed on that tile.

Movement

  • Moves the same way as the Panda (straight line, any distance, no jumping gaps)
  • When you take the Panda action or use Storm weather, you choose to move either the Panda or Miss Panda — not both
  • Miss Panda also moves to a new ⭐ plot when one is placed

Making Babies

If Miss Panda ends her movement on the same plot as the Panda:

  1. The player who moved Miss Panda may return 1 bamboo section of any color to the reserve
  2. Take 1 Panda Baby token of the matching color
  3. Immediately apply the bonus on the back of the token
Only works after Miss Panda moves to the Panda — NOT when the Panda moves to Miss Panda.
The bamboo section comes from your eaten reserve (player board), not from the garden.
👶 Panda Babies

9 tokens total (3 per color). Each has a random bonus on the back:

BonusEffect
💧 IrrigationImmediately take 1 irrigation channel from reserve
🔧 ImprovementImmediately take 1 improvement chip of your choice
🔄 Objective SwapReturn 1 objective card from hand to bottom of its deck, draw 1 from any deck

Scoring

Each Panda Baby token is worth 2 VP at end of game.

Each bonus is available only once per color. Once all 3 tokens of a color are taken, no more babies of that color can be made.
🗺️ New Special Plots (⭐)

All 6 new plots have the ⭐ symbol and follow normal placement rules. They trigger Miss Panda's appearance/movement when placed.

🌈 Kami's Garden

  • Grows all 3 bamboo colors simultaneously
  • When irrigated: 1 section of each color appears
  • Gardener on this plot: all 3 colors grow here + adjacent irrigated plots grow matching color
  • Panda eats only 1 section (player's choice of color)
  • Rain: add only 1 section of your choice
  • Counts as all 3 colors for Plot objectives
  • Improvements apply to all 3 bamboos

⛰️ Sacred Hills (1 green, 1 yellow, 1 pink)

  • When the Gardener stops here: 1 bamboo grows on ALL irrigated plots of that color (not just adjacent)
  • Can receive improvement chips normally

💧 Celestial Pond

  • Functions like the central Pond tile
  • Adjacent plots are automatically irrigated
  • Can start irrigation networks from it
  • No bamboo grows on it
  • Figurines can move through/stop on it

🏡 Gardener's Cabin

  • When the Gardener stops here: draw the top card from each objective deck (3 cards), keep 1, return the other 2 to the bottom of their decks
  • No color — bamboo cannot grow here
  • Figurines can move through/stop on it
🎯 New Objective Cards

Plot Objectives (new)

  • ⭐ center pattern: A ⭐ plot with 2 adjacent plots of specified colors (all irrigated)
  • Color count: Have at least X irrigated plots of a specific color in the garden

Gardener Objectives (new)

  • Height ladder: At least one bamboo of 2, one of 3, and one of 4 sections of the same color
  • Quantity: At least X bamboo shoots of 2+ sections of a specific color

Panda Objectives (new)

  • Require specific bamboo sections on your board AND the Panda must be on a Pond tile (central or Celestial) when completing
Panda-on-Pond rule: If the Panda is already on a Pond tile at the start of your turn, you can complete these objectives without moving it there.
🎲 Optional Variants

These can be played with or without the Chibis expansion:

🍽️ Gourmet

The Panda cannot eat the first (bottom) section of bamboo — only upper sections. A plot must have at least 2 sections for the Panda to eat.

🌈 Diversity

At end of game, each complete set of 3 different objective categories (1 Plot + 1 Gardener + 1 Panda) earns 3 bonus points.
