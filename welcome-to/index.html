Every turn (simultaneous): Flip top card from each of 3 decks → creates 3 number + effect pairs → everyone picks 1 pair → write the house number on any street → optionally use the effect.

The 6 Effects:

📐 Surveyor Build a fence between two houses on any street
🏗️ Real Estate Increase value of one estate size column
🌳 Landscaper Build next park on the same street as your house
🏊 Pool Mfr. Build pool if house is on a pool space
👷 Temp Agency Adjust house number ±1 or ±2
🔁 Bis Duplicate an adjacent house number on any street

Can't place? Take a Building Permit Refusal (penalty). 3rd refusal = game over.

Card peek: Corner icons on each card show the effect on the reverse side — plan ahead!

🏆 Scoring & End Game

The game ends immediately when any player:

  • Completes all 3 City Plans, OR
  • Fills in all houses on all 3 streets, OR
  • Takes their 3rd Building Permit Refusal

⚙️ Quick Setup
  1. Give each player a score sheet and a pen (not pencil — no erasing!).
  2. Shuffle the 81 construction cards into 3 equal decks of 27 cards, number-side face up.
  3. Deal 3 City Plan cards (one n°1, one n°2, one n°3) face up. Use basic or advanced plans.
  4. Flip the top card of each deck onto its discard pile (effect-side up) — this creates the first 3 number+effect pairs.
  5. Everyone simultaneously picks a pair and begins!
Streets have different lengths: Street 1 = 10 houses, Street 2 = 11 houses, Street 3 = 12 houses. Parks are at the end of each street. Pools are pre-printed on specific houses.
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes

Numbers must be strictly ascending left→right.
No duplicates on the same street (unless using Bis). Gaps are fine, but you can paint yourself into a corner fast.

You MUST write a house number every turn.
The effect is optional, but the number is mandatory. If you can't legally place any of the 3 available numbers, you take a Building Permit Refusal.

Bis houses must be adjacent AND in the same estate.
The duplicate must go next to an existing copy of that number. You cannot place a Bis across a fence. You also can't later split a Bis pair with a fence.

Pool Manufacturer only works on pool spaces.
You can write any number on a pool space without the Pool effect — you just don't score the pool. Conversely, using Pool Mfr. on a non-pool space does nothing.

Surveyor fences can go on ANY street.
The fence doesn't have to be on the same street where you wrote your house number. This is the only effect that works cross-street.

Temp Agency range: the number can become 0 (or even 17).
A 2 with Temp Agency can be written as 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4. A 14 can become 16. A 15 can become 17.

City Plan estates get "locked" once claimed.
Mark the estates used to complete a plan — they can't count toward another plan, and you can't split them with fences later.

Simultaneous play — no turns.
Everyone uses the same 3 card pairs each round. There's no turn order in the base game.

Estates ≥ 7 houses score nothing! Only sizes 1–6 have scoring columns. Don't build oversized estates.
Real Estate Agent doesn't score estates directly. It increases the per-house multiplier for a chosen estate size. You still need completed estates of that size to earn points.
⚡ Effects — Detailed Reference
EffectIconRules
Surveyor 📐 Draw a fence between any two adjacent houses on any street (not just where you placed your number). Creates estate boundaries. Each street has implied fences at both ends.
Real Estate Agent 🏗️ Cross off the topmost (lowest) uncrossed value in any one estate-size column (1–6). Increases the per-house multiplier for completed estates of that size at end of game.
Landscaper 🌳 Cross off the leftmost uncrossed park on the same street where you wrote your house number. Parks score increasing points and must be filled left to right.
Pool Manufacturer 🏊 If the house you just numbered has a pre-printed pool symbol, circle it and cross off the next pool space on your score track. No pool symbol = no effect.
Temp Agency 👷 Modify the chosen house number by +1, +2, −1, or −2 (making range 0–17 possible). Cross off a Temp Agency box. Most temps at end of game wins bonus points.
Bis 🔁 After writing your house number, duplicate any existing number on any street into an adjacent empty house (must be same estate — no fence between). Cross off a Bis penalty box. Multiple Bis on the same number = triples, quads, etc.
Strategy: Surveyor and Real Estate Agent are the engine combo. Build fences to create estates, then pump up their value. Landscaper is steady points with no downside. Pools are opportunistic — don't force them.
📋 City Plans

3 City Plan cards are dealt each game (one each of n°1, n°2, n°3). First to complete gets the higher point value; later completers get the lower value.

Basic Plans — require specific combinations of completed estates:

Plan #Requirement Examples1st / 2nd pts
n°1Six 1-house estates / Two 3-house estates / etc.8/4, 8/4, 8/4
n°2One 4-house + one 1-house / Two 2-house + one 3-house / etc.11/6, 10/6, 9/5
n°3One 5-house + one 2-house / Three 3-house / etc.12/7, 12/7, 13/7

Advanced Plans (marked with ★) — more specific requirements:

  • Fill every house on the bottom (longest) street
  • Build the first and last house on each street
  • Build all parks and pools on a specific street
  • Build all parks and pools on any two streets
  • Build 5 Bis duplicates
  • Hire 7 Temp Agency workers
  • All parks + all pools on one street + one roundabout (advanced variant)
Locking estates: When you claim a City Plan, mark the estates you used. Those estates are locked — they can't be used for another plan, and you can't add fences inside them.
Pools are reusable: Houses with pools that were used to complete a City Plan can still count toward other plans. Only the estate boundaries get locked.

Reshuffle option: The first player to complete any City Plan may optionally trigger a one-time reshuffle — leave the 3 top discard cards, shuffle the remaining ~78 cards, and re-deal into 3 decks.

🏘️ Estates & Real Estate Scoring

An estate is a group of 1–6 consecutive houses between two fences, all filled in with numbers. Each street has implied fences at both ends.

Default estate value = 1 pt per house. Each Real Estate Agent effect you use increases the multiplier for one estate size.

Scoring formula: (# of completed estates of size N) × (current multiplier for size N)

Strategy: Pumping Real Estate on a size you have many of is the biggest point swing in the game. Two completed 3-house estates with 3 Real Estate upgrades = 2 × 5 = 10 pts instead of 2 × 3 = 6.
7+ house estates = 0 points. There is no column for estates larger than 6. Always fence off before an estate grows too large.

🔄 Advanced Variant: Roundabouts

Before or after writing a house number, you may build a roundabout on any empty lot:

  1. Draw a circle (⊙) in an empty house space.
  2. Place fences on both sides of the roundabout.
  3. Cross off a roundabout penalty box (negative points at end of game).

A roundabout splits a street into two independent sections. Each section can have the full range of house numbers (0–17). This effectively resets the ascending requirement.

Roundabouts count as filled spaces for City Plans that require filling an entire street. They're a safety valve when you've painted yourself into a corner.
Roundabouts cost points. Each one you build increases your end-game penalty. Use them sparingly.
🎓 Expert & Solo Modes

Expert Mode (drafting):

  1. Combine all cards into one deck, effect-side up.
  2. Deal 3 cards to each player.
  3. Pick 2 cards: one for its house number, one for its effect. Discard both.
  4. Pass the remaining card to the player on your right.
  5. Deal 2 new cards to each player. Repeat.

This adds drafting and more player interaction — you can see what you're passing and what's coming.

Solo Mode:

  1. Shuffle the solo card into the bottom half of the deck.
  2. Draw 3 cards each turn. Pick 2 (one for number, one for effect). Discard all 3.
  3. When the solo card appears, flip all uncompleted City Plans to their lower-scoring "approved" side.
  4. Game also ends when the deck runs out.
  5. 6+ temps = 7 pts. Fewer than 6 = 0 pts.
