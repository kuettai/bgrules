🐣 Welcome To…: Spring Neighbourhood

Expansion · 1–50 players · ~25 min · Designer: Benoit Turpin · Deep Water Games (2019)

📦 What's New

Spring adds an Easter egg hunt layer on top of the base game. The expansion includes:

  • 50 new score sheets — houses now have Easter eggs printed on them (1 or 2 eggs per house)
  • Egg collection mechanic — collect eggs by writing "loop" numbers on houses with eggs
  • 3 new City Plan cards — tied to egg collection milestones
  • New scoring row — bonus points for total eggs collected (big bonuses at 14 and 18)
Same cards, new sheets. You use the same 81 construction cards and effects from the base game — only the score sheets and City Plans change.
🔀 Rule Changes — Egg Collection

Houses on the Spring score sheet have Easter eggs printed on them (some have 1 egg, some have 2, many have none). When you write a house number on a space with eggs, you collect those eggs only if the number contains a "loop" — a closed circle in its digit(s).

What is a "loop" number? Any digit that contains an enclosed circle in its written form: 0, 6, 8, 9. The number 8 has two loops; 0 also has a loop shape that counts for 2.

0 🥚🥚 2 eggs
6 🥚 1 egg
8 🥚🥚 2 eggs
9 🥚 1 egg
10 🥚🥚 2 eggs
16 🥚 1 egg

How it works:

  1. Write a house number on a space that has printed eggs
  2. If the number is a loop number (0, 6, 8, 9, 10, or 16), you circle the egg(s) on that house
  3. Mark the collected eggs on your egg track at the bottom of the sheet
Non-loop numbers don't collect! Writing a 3, 5, 7, 11, 12, etc. on a house with eggs means those eggs are wasted — you can never go back and collect them.
Temp Agency interaction: You can use Temp Agency (±1 or ±2) to adjust a number into a loop number. For example, turning a 7 into a 6, 8, or 9 to collect eggs on that house.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Replace the base game score sheets with the Spring score sheets
  2. Replace the 3 base City Plan cards with the 3 Spring City Plan cards
  3. Use the same 81 construction cards — shuffle into 3 decks as normal
  4. All other setup is identical to the base game
That's it. Same cards, same effects, same turn structure. The only physical change is the sheets and City Plans.
🏆 Scoring Changes

All base game scoring categories still apply. The Spring sheet adds an egg bonus track:

Total Eggs CollectedBonus Points
0–13 eggsPoints scale up gradually
14 eggs🎯 Large bonus milestone
15–17 eggsContinues scaling
18 eggs🎯 Huge bonus — maximum egg score

New City Plans are also tied to egg collection — requiring you to collect a certain number of eggs or collect eggs in specific patterns across streets.

Eggs are pure bonus. They add to your score on top of everything else. Hitting the 14 or 18 thresholds is a significant point swing — plan your loop numbers carefully.
Don't neglect base scoring! Eggs are tempting, but estates, parks, and pools still make up the bulk of your score. Balance egg hunting with your core strategy.
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes

Only 6 numbers collect eggs: 0, 6, 8, 9, 10, 16.
Numbers like 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 have no loops — they never collect eggs, even on egg spaces.

Placing a non-loop number on an egg space wastes those eggs permanently.
You can't come back later. Once a house has a number, it's done. Think before you write!

The number of eggs you collect depends on the house, not the number.
A house with 1 printed egg gives 1 egg regardless of whether you write 0 (2 loops) or 6 (1 loop). The loop count on the number determines if you collect; the house determines how many.

Temp Agency can save egg spaces.
If you're about to place a non-loop number on an egg house, consider using Temp Agency to shift into 0, 6, 8, or 9 range. A 7→8 or 7→6 adjustment is a common play.

Bis duplicates can collect eggs too.
If you Bis a loop number onto an adjacent house that has eggs, you collect those eggs.

Don't tunnel-vision on eggs. The expansion is designed so that egg collection creates interesting tension with number placement — but the ascending order rule still applies. Forcing a 0 or 8 into a bad position just for eggs can cost you more points than the eggs are worth.
