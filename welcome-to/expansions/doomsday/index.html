☢️ Welcome To…: Doomsday

Expansion · 1–50 players · ~25 min · Requires Welcome To… base game

📦 What's New
  • Bomb shelters (bunkers) — certain houses now have bunker icons in their gardens, similar to pool placement in the base game
  • Resident saving — when you write a number on a house with a bunker, write the same number on the bunker; that number = how many residents you save
  • New score sheet — Doomsday-themed pad with bunker spaces printed along the streets
  • 3 new City Plan cards — focused on shelter-related objectives and fence placement for estate sizes
💡 This is the most beginner-friendly expansion — the core flip-and-write gameplay is barely changed, just bunkers layered on top.
🔀 Rule Changes

Shelter Mechanics

  • Bunkers are pre-printed on certain houses across all three streets (they do not replace pools)
  • When you write a house number on a space that has a bunker, you must also write that same number on the bunker
  • The bunker number represents how many residents you save in that shelter
  • Houses without bunkers work exactly as in the base game

Resident Counting

  • At game end, total the bunker numbers on each street separately
  • Also total all bunker numbers across your entire neighbourhood

First to 50 Race

  • The first player to reach a total of 50 saved residents (across all streets combined) earns a big bonus

City Plan Cards

  • 3 new City Plan cards replace the base game plans
  • Mostly focused on fencing off particular estate sizes — standard completion rules apply
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Replace the base game score sheets with the Doomsday score sheets
  2. Replace the 3 base City Plan cards with the 3 Doomsday City Plan cards
  3. Use the same 3 construction card decks from the base game — no card changes
💡 Everything else stays the same: same card decks, same turn structure, same action choices.
🏆 Scoring Changes

All base game scoring still applies. The Doomsday expansion adds up to 40 bonus points:

BonusPointsCondition
Most saved — Street 1 10 Highest total bunker value on street 1
Most saved — Street 2 10 Highest total bunker value on street 2
Most saved — Street 3 10 Highest total bunker value on street 3
The Optimist 10 Player with the fewest total residents saved (all streets combined)
⚠️ No single player can earn all 40 points — you can't have both the most per street AND the fewest overall. This is intentional balance.

Tie-breaking

  • If tied for most saved on a street, all tied players receive the 10-point bonus
  • If tied for fewest total saved (Optimist), all tied players receive the 10-point bonus
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
  • Bunker number must match house number — you can't write a different value on the bunker. It's always the same number you wrote on the house
  • Higher numbers = more residents saved but also makes it harder to complete contiguous estates for City Plans and fencing bonuses. There's a real tension here
  • Bunkers don't replace pools — a house can potentially have both a pool and a bunker
  • You still score base game points normally — parks, pools, estates, bis penalties, etc. all still apply
  • City Plans are replaced, not added — use only the 3 Doomsday plans, not the base game ones
⚠️ 2-player swinginess: With only 2 players, the 10-point street bonuses create large point swings. One player can dominate all 3 streets (30 pts) while the other gets only the Optimist bonus (10 pts) — a 20-point gap from shelters alone. Be aware this can feel lopsided at 2p.
💡 Strategy tip: Don't go all-in on shelters. Winning 1–2 street bonuses while keeping your estates and parks scoring healthy is usually better than chasing all three streets.
