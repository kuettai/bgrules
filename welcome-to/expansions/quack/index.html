🦆 Welcome To… Quack

Free Print-and-Play Expansion · Blue Cocker (2020) · New score sheet + 3 City Plan cards

📦 What's New

Two families of ducks — Yellow and Black — are lost in the streets of your city. Reunite the ducklings with their parents by circling them and building unbroken chains back to the parent duck.

New Components (Print & Play)

  • New score sheet — streets now have ducklings and parent ducks printed between houses
  • 3 new N°3 City Plan cards — replace the base game's N°3 City Plan
  • All base game cards and construction decks are reused as-is

Core Duckling Mechanic

  • Ducklings sit between houses (horizontally) or between streets (vertically)
  • Circle a duckling when both adjacent houses on either side are numbered
  • Connect circled ducklings in an unbroken chain of circled ducklings + numbered houses back to the matching parent duck to score
  • Fences can be built where a duckling sits — fences don't block duckling chains
💡 All base game rules still apply normally. This expansion only adds the duckling layer on top.
🔀 Rule Changes

Circling Ducklings

  • A duckling is "found" (circled) as soon as both houses adjacent to it are numbered — this happens automatically, no action cost
  • Ducklings between streets require a numbered house above and below

Connecting to Parents

  • A circled duckling is "brought back" only when there is an uninterrupted chain of circled ducklings and numbered houses connecting it to its colour's parent duck
  • Yellow ducklings → Yellow parent; Black ducklings → Black parent

Three Scoring Phases (triggered during play)

  1. First colour complete — first player to connect ALL ducklings of one colour to their parent announces it → every player scores 1 pt per duckling of that colour connected to its parent
  2. Second colour complete — same trigger for the other colour → all players score that colour's connected ducklings
  3. Both colours complete — first player to connect both colours → all players score 1 pt per duckling of either colour connected to its parent
⚠️ Ducklings connected to their parent can score twice — once in their colour phase and again in the "both colours" phase.

Catch-Up Mechanic

When a scoring phase triggers, every player scores — not just the one who triggered it. This rewards all players who've been building chains, even if they weren't first.

💡 If the same player triggers both the 2nd colour and the "both colours" phase simultaneously, perform both scoring phases back-to-back immediately.
⚙️ Setup Changes

Print & Play Instructions

  1. Download the free PnP files from Blue Cocker / BGG
  2. Print the new score sheet (one per player) — replaces the base game sheet
  3. Print and cut the 3 new N°3 City Plan cards

Game Setup

  • Use the base game's construction card decks — no changes
  • Pick a N°1 and N°2 City Plan from the base game as normal
  • Replace the N°3 City Plan with one of the 3 Quack City Plans
  • Each player takes a Quack score sheet instead of the standard one

End of Game

If nobody has triggered the "both colours" phase by game end, perform it anyway — all players score 1 pt per duckling of either colour connected to its parent. Unconnected ducklings are lost for good.

⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
  • Circled ≠ Connected — a duckling can be circled (found) but not connected to its parent. City Plans only need circled; scoring phases need connected.
  • Everyone scores — when a scoring phase triggers, ALL players score their connected ducklings, not just the triggering player.
  • Fences don't block chains — you can build a fence on a duckling's space without breaking the chain.
  • Ducklings score twice — once in their colour phase, once in the "both colours" phase. Don't forget the second scoring.
  • Automatic circling — ducklings are circled the moment both adjacent houses are numbered. Don't forget to check after every house placement.
  • N°3 City Plan only — the Quack City Plans replace only the N°3 slot. N°1 and N°2 come from the base game.
  • End-game safety net — even if nobody completes both colours, the 3rd scoring phase still happens at game end.
