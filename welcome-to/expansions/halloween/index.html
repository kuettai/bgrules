📦 What's New
  • Halloween score sheet — new neighborhood layout with 13 houses marked with candy & ghost icons (always paired together)
  • Candy corn bonuses — first-to-achieve race bonuses worth 5 / 10 / 15 points for collecting 4 / 6 / 9 candy
  • Ghost bonuses — first-to-achieve race bonuses granting bonus actions (parks, pools, or penalty-free bis) for collecting 4 / 6 / 9 ghosts
  • Halloween City Plan cards — new themed city plans replacing the base game set
  • Spooky artwork — trick-or-treat themed neighborhood with haunted houses
🔀 Rule Changes

Trick or Treat — Candy & Ghosts

13 houses on the sheet have a candy/ghost pair in front. When you write a number on one of these houses, you must circle either the candy or the ghost — never both.

First-to-Achieve Bonuses (Race)

When you reach a candy or ghost threshold, announce it immediately to claim the bonus. Once claimed, other players cross it off — it's gone.

  • If two players trigger the same bonus simultaneously, both receive it
  • A lower-tier bonus can still be claimed if unclaimed, even after a higher tier is reached
  • Max 1 ghost bonus + 1 candy bonus per player

Push Your Luck

You're incentivized to commit heavily to one type (candy or ghosts) to reach the highest tier. But going all-in means you may miss out on the other type entirely, and slower players risk getting shut out of bonuses.

💡 Ghost bonuses are often more powerful than candy — free parks/pools score points and help complete city plans. But candy is guaranteed points with no decision overhead.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  • Replace base game score sheets with the Halloween neighborhood sheets
  • Replace base game City Plan cards with the Halloween City Plan cards
  • The three construction card decks remain unchanged — same cards as the base game
💡 Everything else (card decks, turn structure, action selection) stays the same as the base game.
🏆 Scoring Changes

All base game scoring categories still apply. Additionally:

  • Candy bonus — add 5, 10, or 15 points if you claimed a candy bonus
  • Ghost bonus — no direct points, but the free parks/pools/bis actions contribute to their respective scoring rows
  • Parks and pools gained via ghost bonuses count normally toward park and pool scoring
  • Penalty-free bis from ghost bonuses does not add to your bis penalty total
⚠️ Ghost bonus actions are applied immediately when claimed — don't forget to mark the parks/pools right away.
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
  • Must circle one — you cannot skip the candy/ghost choice on a marked house. You must pick one.
  • Max 1 of each bonus type — even if you collect all 13, you can only claim one ghost bonus and one candy bonus total.
  • Claimed = gone — once a bonus tier is claimed by any player, it's unavailable to everyone else. Cross it off immediately.
  • Simultaneous triggers — if two players hit the same threshold on the same turn, both get it. Don't forget this edge case.
  • Lower tiers stay available — if nobody claimed the 4-candy bonus but someone claims the 6-candy bonus, the 4-candy bonus is still up for grabs.
  • Ghost actions are immediate — apply parks/pools/bis right when you claim, not at end of game.
  • In 4-player games — if everyone chases the same type, one player may get completely shut out of bonuses. Diversify if the table is competitive.
