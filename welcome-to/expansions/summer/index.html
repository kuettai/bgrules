🍦 Welcome To…: Summer Neighbourhood

Expansion · 1–50 players · ~25 min · Designer: Benoit Turpin & Alexis Allard (2019) · BGG 7.4 · Weight 1.67/5

📦 What's New
  • Ice Cream Truck — a truck travels along each street; writing house numbers in the truck's direction earns scoops
  • New score sheet — summer-themed art with ice cream cone tracks on each street
  • 3 new City Plan cards — summer-themed objectives that replace the base game plans
  • Ice cream cone bonuses — each street has a cone bonus you can unlock
💡 All base-game rules still apply — this expansion only adds the ice cream truck layer on top.
🔀 Rule Changes — Ice Cream Truck

Truck Direction

Each street has an ice cream truck that travels in a fixed direction (left → right or right → left), shown by an arrow on the sheet. The truck starts at one end and advances toward the other.

How the Truck Moves

When you write a house number on a street, check if the numbers you've written so far on that street are ascending in the same direction the truck travels. Each consecutive house number written in the truck's direction advances the truck one space.

Scoop Scoring

As the truck advances, it passes scoop markers on the track:

ScoopsMeaning
🍨 SingleTruck passes the 1st marker — score single scoop points
🍨🍨 DoubleTruck passes the 2nd marker — score double scoop bonus
🍨🍨🍨 TripleTruck reaches the end — score triple scoop bonus

The Core Tension

House numbers must still be in ascending order left-to-right (base game rule), but the truck may travel right-to-left. You need numbers ascending for placement and aligned with the truck direction for scoops — these can conflict!

💡 On streets where the truck goes right → left, higher numbers placed on the left side first help the truck advance, but limit your future placement options.

Ice Cream Cone Bonus

Each street also has an ice cream cone bonus. If you fill in enough houses on that street (reaching the cone threshold), you earn extra points for each scoop collected on that street.

⚙️ Setup Changes
  • Replace the base game score sheets with the Summer score sheets
  • Replace the 3 base game City Plan cards with the 3 Summer City Plan cards
  • Use the same construction card decks (numbers + actions) from the base game — no changes
💡 The Summer sheet is a drop-in replacement. No extra tokens or components needed beyond the new sheets and plans.
🏆 Scoring Changes

End-game scoring is the same as the base game plus ice cream points:

CategoryChange
City PlansUse the 3 Summer plans instead of base plans
Parks, Pools, Temps, Fences, BisNo change — score as normal
🍦 Ice Cream ScoopsNEW — sum all scoop points earned across all 3 streets
🍦 Cone BonusesNEW — if you unlocked a street's cone bonus, multiply scoops on that street
⚠️ Ice cream points can be significant — don't ignore the truck! A full triple-scoop on all streets is a big swing.
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
  • Truck direction ≠ number order. Numbers always go low → high left-to-right (base rule). The truck direction is independent — read the arrow on the sheet carefully.
  • The truck only advances when numbers are written in its direction. Writing a number on the "wrong" side of the street still places the house, but doesn't move the truck.
  • You can't move the truck backward. Once it passes a scoop marker, that marker is locked in.
  • Cone bonus is per-street. You must meet the threshold on each street individually — it's not cumulative across streets.
  • City Plans are different. Don't accidentally mix Summer plans with base game plans — they're designed for this sheet.
⚠️ The most common mistake: forgetting that the truck direction on the middle street may differ from the top and bottom streets. Check each street's arrow!
