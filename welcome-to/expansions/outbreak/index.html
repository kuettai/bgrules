🧟 Welcome To…: Outbreak

Thematic Neighborhood Expansion · Designers: Alexis Allard & Benoit Turpin · Deep Water Games (2019)

📦 What's New

A zombie horde is invading your neighbourhood! Outbreak layers a survival theme onto the base Welcome To… flip-and-write, adding three new mechanics:

🧱 Barricades

The new player sheet has incomplete barricade sections along the bottom of each street, in front of certain houses. When you choose a number + fence action and write it onto a house that has a barricade slot, you may also draw in that barricade section — reinforcing your defences.

🧟 Zombie Horde

Three zombie cards are shuffled into one of the three construction decks. When a zombie card is revealed, the horde advances from both ends of every street, invading the first non-barricaded house they reach. The zombies attack three times during the game.

🔫 Bullets (Ammo)

You start with 1 bullet. Additional bullets are earned by advancing your real estate values to stages marked with bullet icons (up to 6 extra available). When zombies advance, you may spend 1 bullet to block the horde on a single street for that wave.

⏱️ Time Pressure

Because zombies are shuffled into the deck, you're racing to get barricades built and ammo collected before the horde arrives. Timing your fence actions is critical.

🔀 Rule Changes

Barricade Building

  • When you pick a number + fence combo and write the number on a house with a barricade slot, you may fill in that barricade section
  • Barricades are drawn along the bottom of the street — they block zombie advancement from that direction
  • You still place the normal fence as usual; the barricade is an additional benefit

Zombie Advancement

  • 3 zombie cards are shuffled into one construction deck
  • When revealed, zombies advance from both ends of each street simultaneously
  • They invade the first house not protected by a completed barricade
  • Invaded houses still count for numbering but may affect scoring

Bullet Collection & Spending

  • Start with 1 bullet (pre-circled on your sheet)
  • Earn more by advancing real estate values past bullet-icon thresholds (6 available)
  • When zombies advance, spend 1 bullet → block the horde on one street for that wave
  • Cross off the bullet symbol when spent
  • Remaining unspent bullets score nothing at end of game — use them or lose them

New City Plan Cards

  • 2 of the City Plan cards focus on completing barricades on a street
  • 1 City Plan card requires spending 5 bullets
  • These replace some base-game City Plans — use only the Outbreak set
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Use the Outbreak player sheets (with barricade slots and bullet track)
  2. Shuffle the 3 zombie cards into one of the three construction card decks
  3. Replace base-game City Plan cards with the Outbreak City Plan cards
  4. Each player circles their starting bullet on the sheet
  5. All other setup follows the base game (3 decks of construction cards, etc.)
💡 Shuffle the zombie cards well into the deck — if they cluster near the top, the game becomes brutally hard.
🏆 Scoring Changes

Base-game scoring (houses, parks, pools, real estate, City Plans, bis penalties) still applies. Outbreak adds:

⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
  • Barricades require the fence action — you can only draw a barricade when you've chosen a number + fence pairing, not with any other action
  • Zombies attack from both ends — unlike other expansions, you must defend two directions per street, not one
  • Bullets don't score — new players often hoard ammo expecting end-game points. There are none. Spend them strategically
  • Bullet supply is luck-dependent — you earn bullets via real estate advancement, which depends on card draws. Don't count on getting all 6
  • Letting zombies in can be optimal — sometimes it's point-efficient to sacrifice the edges of a street rather than waste fence actions on barricades. Weigh the trade-off
  • 1 bullet = 1 street for 1 wave — a bullet doesn't protect all streets, just the one you choose when zombies advance
  • Use Outbreak City Plans only — don't mix base-game City Plan cards with the Outbreak set
💡 Prioritise barricades on the streets where your high-value houses are. Losing cheap edge houses hurts less than losing a completed estate.
