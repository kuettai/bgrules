🏆 Winning Condition

First player to collect 3 Royal Relics wins and becomes ruler of the Turnip Kingdom!

🏅 8 turnips Scepter (1st)
→
👑 9 turnips Crown (2nd)
→
🪑 10 turnips Throne (3rd)

Relics must be purchased in order (Scepter → Crown → Throne) using a Merchant card.

Turnips can be spent from both your Stockpile and your Bank.

If you can afford a relic when you play a Merchant, you MUST buy it. You only use the Merchant's alternate effect (buy a market card) if you can't afford the next relic.

Tiebreaker: If multiple players get their 3rd relic in the same round, the player with the most turnips remaining wins. Then most cards in hand.

⚙️ Quick Setup
  1. Each player takes 4 starting cards (marked with ✱): Farmer, Wall, Raider, Merchant.
  2. Each player takes a Bank card (use "Duel" side for 2 players).
  3. Each player takes 2 turnip tokens: 1 on the Bank card, 1 in their Stockpile.
  4. Place all Relic tokens and remaining Turnip tokens in a central Supply.
  5. Shuffle the 20 Market cards face-down. Reveal the top 4 as the Market row.
  6. Seat players in a circle — you only interact with your immediate neighbors.
2-Player Duel: Flip the Bank card to the "Duel" side (only 4 bank spaces, cheaper relics). Play 2 cards: one for "now" and one for "later" — the front card resolves this turn, the back card next turn.
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes

Merchant MUST buy a relic if you can afford it.
You don't get to choose — if you have enough turnips for your next relic when your Merchant resolves, you must purchase it. The "buy a card" option is only available when you can't afford a relic.

Relics must be bought in order.
Scepter (8) → Crown (9) → Throne (10). You can't skip ahead or buy out of sequence.

Stolen turnips come from the Stockpile only.
Raiders steal from your neighbor's Stockpile (the loose turnips), NOT from their Bank — unless a card specifically says otherwise.

Banked turnips are safe (usually).
Turnips on your Bank card can't be stolen by normal Raiders. Only certain Market cards can steal from the Bank.

You can spend Bank turnips to buy things.
When buying relics or market cards, you can pay from both your Stockpile and your Bank.

You only interact with your immediate neighbors.
In a 4–5 player game, you cannot affect players who aren't sitting next to you. This is by design.

Cards go back to your hand every round.
Unlike a deck-builder, you always have access to all your cards. New market cards are immediately available next round.

Skipped cards stay out for a round. Some Market cards can "skip" an opponent's card. That card stays face-up and can't be used next round — it returns to hand the round after.

Market cards are limited.
Once the Market deck runs out, no more cards can be purchased. Refill the Market row only when a card is bought.

Same card types can be played to both neighbors.
You can play Farmer left AND Farmer right if you want — you're not forced to play different cards.

Sitting next to a weak player is a huge advantage. Since you only fight neighbors, the "kingmaker" problem is real in 4–5 player games. Try to balance seating if possible.

🏦 The Bank

Each player has a Bank card that safely stores turnips away from Raiders.

  • 3+ players: Bank holds up to 5 turnips
  • 2 players (Duel): Bank holds up to 4 turnips

Turnips in the Bank cannot be stolen by normal Raiders — only by specific Market cards (like the Burglar).

You can spend Bank turnips when buying relics or market cards.

Banking is done via card effects only. You can't just move turnips into your Bank whenever you want — you need a Wall or similar card effect that says "Bank."

The Bank card also shows the resolve order (Farmer → Wall → Raider → Merchant) as a handy reminder.

⚔️ 2-Player Duel Rules

The 2-player game uses modified rules to add a programming element:

  1. Flip Bank cards to the "Duel" side (4 bank spaces, cheaper relics).
  2. Each turn, play 2 cards against your opponent — one in front ("now") and one behind ("later").
  3. Only the front card is revealed and resolved each round.
  4. The back card slides forward to become next round's front card, and you add a new card behind it.

Duel Relic Costs:

🏅 6 turnips Scepter
→
👑 7 turnips Crown
→
🪑 8 turnips Throne
The programming twist makes 2-player games more strategic — you're committing to a card one turn in advance, so reading your opponent becomes even more important.
💡 Strategy Tips
  • Buy market cards early. More cards = more options = harder to predict. The market is limited, so don't wait.
  • Bank aggressively. Banked turnips are safe from most theft. A full bank (5 turnips) plus a few in your stockpile can buy a relic in one shot.
  • Watch your neighbor's turnip count. A player sitting on a pile of turnips is likely to play a Merchant — raid them!
  • The Merchant is a gamble. It's the only path to victory but leaves you completely vulnerable to Raiders. Time it when your neighbor is likely to Farm or Wall.
  • Don't be predictable. If you always Farm, your neighbors will always Raid you. Mix it up.
  • Pay attention to what cards your neighbors have bought. Knowing their hand helps you predict their plays.
  • In 4–5 player games, make deals. "I'll go easy on you if you attack the leader" — just remember, deals aren't binding!
The game is self-balancing: Players with fewer turnips get to pick first from the market when buying cards. Leading players become targets for their neighbors.
