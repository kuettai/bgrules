🎃 Dark Arts & Crafts

Village Pillage Expansion · 2–5 players · 15–30 min · Designers: Peter C. Hayward & Tom Lang · Jellybean Games (2025)

📦 What's New

🟠 Pumpkins — The Fabled Second Vegetable

A brand-new resource alongside turnips. The expansion includes 30 pumpkin tokens that function as a separate currency used exclusively for casting spells.

🔮 Spell Cards

A unique deck of powerful, game-changing spell cards. Spend pumpkins to cast spells that can disrupt opponents, protect yourself, or bend the rules in your favor. Each spell is one-use.

🛒 New Market Cards

An all-new set of pumpkin-themed market cards featuring characters focused on gathering and stealing pumpkins. These are shuffled into (or replace portions of) the market alongside the base game's turnip-focused cards.

🃏 New Starting Hand

For the first time in Village Pillage, each player receives a new set of starter cards that replace the base game starting hand. These new starters interact with both turnips and pumpkins.

🎩 The Very Big Hat

A new tiebreaker token. The player wearing the Very Big Hat goes first in all circumstances. But beware — the hat is stealable! If you successfully steal turnips from the hat-holder, you steal the hat too.

🔀 Rule Changes

Two-Resource Economy

The game now runs on two separate currencies:

  • Turnips 🥕 — still used to buy market cards and relics (win condition unchanged: collect 3 relics)
  • Pumpkins 🎃 — gathered via new market cards and spent exclusively to cast spells

Pumpkins and turnips are not interchangeable — you cannot spend pumpkins to buy relics or turnips to cast spells (unless a specific spell says otherwise).

Casting Spells

After all card matchups resolve each round, players may cast spells by paying the pumpkin cost shown on the spell card. Spells are one-use and discarded after casting.

💡 Spells resolve after normal card resolution — plan your card plays knowing you (or your neighbors) might cast a spell afterward.

New Starting Hand

Replace each player's base game starter cards with the Dark Arts starter set. The new starters include ways to interact with both turnips and pumpkins from the very first turn.

The Very Big Hat

  • At game start, give the hat to a random player
  • The hat-holder resolves first whenever turn order matters
  • When a Raider successfully steals turnips from the hat-holder, the Raider also steals the hat
  • The hat has no other game effect — it's purely a tiebreaker and first-player marker

Mixed Magic Mode (6+ Players)

If you have extra player packs, you can play at higher player counts. Some players use the original base game starter hand while others use the Dark Arts starters. All players share the combined market and spell deck.

⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Replace each player's base game starting hand with the Dark Arts starter cards
  2. Shuffle the new pumpkin market cards into the market deck (or follow the rulebook's guidance on which base cards to remove)
  3. Place the spell deck face-down in the center of the table
  4. Place the 30 pumpkin tokens in a supply next to the turnip supply
  5. Give the Very Big Hat token to a random player
  6. Set up relics and turnips as normal per base game rules
💡 Keep pumpkins and turnips in separate piles — they're easy to mix up mid-game.
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
⚠️ Pumpkins ≠ Turnips. You cannot spend pumpkins to buy relics or market cards. They are only for casting spells.
⚠️ Hat theft is automatic. If you steal turnips from the hat-holder via a Raider, you must take the hat — it's not optional.
⚠️ Replace the starting hand entirely. Don't mix base game starters with Dark Arts starters for the same player (unless playing Mixed Magic mode at 6+).
⚠️ Spells resolve after card matchups. Don't cast spells during card resolution — wait until all matchups are done.
⚠️ Win condition is unchanged. You still need 3 relics bought with turnips. Pumpkins and spells are a means to an end, not an alternate victory path.
