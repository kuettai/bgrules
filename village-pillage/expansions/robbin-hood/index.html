🏹 Village Pillage: Robbin' Hood

Expansion · 2–5 players · ~20–30 min · Designer: Peter C. Hayward · Jellybean Games (2023)

📦 What's New
  • 32 new market cards — over a dozen unique card types to buy and add to your hand, making every game different.
  • Rich/Poor mechanic — cards now have different effects depending on whether your opponent is richer or poorer than you (based on turnip count).
  • Arrow (⟶) timing — some cards are marked with an arrow, meaning they resolve after all other cards of their type. They lurk in the shadows until it's time to strike.
  • More stealing, more interaction — the expansion doubles down on what Village Pillage does best: raiding your neighbors for turnips.
🔀 Rule Changes

Rich / Poor Determination

When you play a card with a Rich/Poor effect against a neighbor, compare turnip counts:

ConditionOpponent StatusEffect Used
Opponent has more turnips than youRich 💰The card's "Rich" effect triggers
Opponent has fewer turnips than youPoor 🪙The card's "Poor" effect triggers
Opponent has the same turnips as youNeitherNeither Rich nor Poor effect triggers
💡 Rich/Poor is always relative to you. The same opponent can be Rich on your left side and Poor on your right side if your turnip counts differ per matchup.

Arrow (⟶) Card Timing

Cards marked with an arrow resolve after all other cards of the same type have resolved. This lets them "lurk in the shadows" — you see what happens first, then the arrow card strikes.

  1. Resolve all non-arrow cards of that type as normal.
  2. Then resolve all arrow-marked cards of that type.
💡 Arrow cards are powerful because they act with more information — but opponents can anticipate them once they know you own one.

New Card Types

The 32 new cards include new market cards that combine the base game's card types (Farmer, Raider, Wall, Merchant) with the Rich/Poor mechanic and arrow timing. They shuffle into the existing market supply.

⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Set up the base game as normal (starting hands, turnip supply, relics).
  2. Shuffle all 32 Robbin' Hood market cards into the existing market deck.
  3. That's it — no other changes to setup. The new cards appear as players buy from the market during play.
💡 The expansion is fully compatible with the base game market — just shuffle in and go.
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
⚠️ Rich/Poor is per-matchup, not global. You compare turnips separately against each neighbor. Your left neighbor might be Rich while your right neighbor is Poor.
⚠️ Equal turnips = neither Rich nor Poor. If you and your opponent have the same number of turnips, the Rich/Poor effect does not trigger. The card may still have a base effect depending on the specific card.
⚠️ Arrow cards resolve after, not instead. An arrow card still resolves — it just waits. Don't skip it; resolve it after all non-arrow cards of that type.
⚠️ Turnip count is checked at resolution time. Compare turnips when the Rich/Poor card actually resolves, not when it was played. Turnip counts may have changed from earlier card resolutions in the same round.
⚠️ Don't forget arrow timing with Rich/Poor. If an arrow card also has a Rich/Poor effect, the Rich/Poor comparison happens when the arrow card resolves (after other cards), which may give a different result than if it had resolved earlier.
