🕯️ Village Pillage: Cultists

Mini-expansion · Big Box exclusive · Jellybean Games · Designer: Peter C. Hayward

"Odd fellows with peculiar powers."

📦 What's New

The Cultists expansion adds a set of new Cultist cards to the market. Each cultist has a unique, strange power that sets it apart from standard villagers.

🕯️ Cultist Cards

New market cards with bizarre abilities. Each cultist has its own peculiar power that triggers when played against a neighbor.

📈 Scaling Power

The more cultists played in a single round (across all players), the more powerful their effects become. A lone cultist is odd — a table full of them is terrifying.

🔮 Cultist vs Cultist

When two cultists are played directly against each other, strange things occur — unique interactions that don't follow normal card resolution rules.

🛒 Market Addition

Cultist cards are shuffled into (or added alongside) the market deck. Buy them with your Merchant like any other market card.

💡 Cultists reward coordination — or at least paying attention to what your neighbors are buying. If multiple players stock up on cultists, every round gets wilder.
🔀 Rule Changes
  • Cultists are played like any card — choose one for your left neighbor and one for your right, then reveal simultaneously as normal.
  • Scaling mechanic: After all cards are revealed, count the total number of cultist cards played this round. Each cultist's power scales based on that count — more cultists = stronger effects.
  • Cultist mirror match: When a cultist is played directly against another cultist, resolve the special cultist-vs-cultist interaction instead of the normal effect. Check each cultist card for its mirror text.
  • Resolution order: Cultist effects resolve in the same phase as the card type they most resemble (e.g., a cultist that steals resolves in the Raider phase). Check each card for timing.
  • No change to win condition: You still win by collecting 3 relics.
⚠️ Cultist scaling counts all cultists on the table that round — not just yours. You benefit from other players' cultists too.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Set up the base game as normal (starting hands, turnip supply, relics, market).
  2. Shuffle the Cultist cards into the market deck, or lay them out as additional market options (check your card count and preferred style).
  3. No changes to starting hands — cultists are only acquired through the Merchant during play.
💡 For a first game with Cultists, consider laying them face-up beside the market so players can read the new powers before buying.
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes

❌ Forgetting the count

Cultist power scales with total cultists played that round. Don't forget to count all of them before resolving.

❌ Normal resolution on mirrors

When two cultists face each other, use the special mirror interaction — not the standard effect.

❌ Hoarding cultists

Cultists are powerful but situational. A hand full of cultists with no Farmers or Walls leaves you vulnerable to basic Raiders.

❌ Ignoring opponent purchases

If your neighbor buys a cultist, you need to plan for it. Cultist-vs-cultist mirrors can backfire if you're not ready.
