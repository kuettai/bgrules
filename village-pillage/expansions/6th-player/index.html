🥕 Village Pillage: Expansion Pack

6th Player + Promo Cards · Jellybean Games (2019) · Requires base game

📦 What's New

This mini-expansion raises the player count from 2–5 to 2–6 and adds 4 promo market cards with unique effects.

6th Player Components

🃏 4 Starting Cards Farmer, Wall, Raider, Merchant
🏦 1 Bank Card With Duel side

3 Promo Relics

🐔 Chicken Scepter Cosmetic variant
🐔 Chicken Crown Cosmetic variant
🐔 Chicken Throne Cosmetic variant
The chicken relics are purely cosmetic. They function identically to the standard relics — use whichever set you prefer, or mix and match for fun.

4 Promo Market Cards

One new card of each type, shuffled into the Market deck:

🌾 Shepherd Farmer type
🧱 Monk Wall type
⚔️ Turncoat Raider type
🛒 Smuggler Merchant type
🔀 Rule Changes

The core rules are unchanged. The expansion simply adds components for a 6th player and new market cards.

  • All base game rules apply as written.
  • Resolve order remains: Farmer → Wall → Raider → Merchant.
  • Win condition is still 3 relics (Scepter 8, Crown 9, Throne 10).
  • Each player still plays 1 card toward each neighbor per round.
With 6 players, the game plays slightly longer but each player still only interacts with their two immediate neighbors. The circle just gets bigger.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Give the 6th player their 4 starting cards (Farmer, Wall, Raider, Merchant) and Bank card.
  2. Give them 2 turnip tokens (1 on Bank, 1 in Stockpile) — same as everyone else.
  3. Seat all 6 players in a circle.
  4. Shuffle the 4 promo market cards (Shepherd, Monk, Turncoat, Smuggler) into the Market deck.
  5. Reveal the top 4 cards as the Market row (now drawn from a 24-card deck instead of 20).
The promo market cards can be used at any player count (2–6). You don't need 6 players to use them — just shuffle them in whenever you want more variety.
🃏 New Cards

Each promo card is a Market card with "wackier effects" than the base game cards. They follow the same type system and resolve in the standard order.

Card Type Description
🌾 Shepherd Farmer A Farmer-type card with a unique twist on turnip generation. Resolves in the Farmer phase (1st). Like all Farmers, vulnerable to Raiders.
🧱 Monk Wall A Wall-type card with a defensive ability. Resolves in the Wall phase (2nd). Protects against Raiders like other Walls.
⚔️ Turncoat Raider A Raider-type card with a twist on stealing. Resolves in the Raider phase (3rd). Blocked by Walls like other Raiders.
🛒 Smuggler Merchant A Merchant-type card. Must buy a relic if affordable (mandatory rule still applies). Has a unique alternate effect when you can't afford a relic. Resolves in the Merchant phase (4th).
These cards are purchased from the Market like any other Market card — spend turnips via a Merchant to add them to your hand. They're not starting cards.
Card-specific effects: Each promo card has unique matchup effects printed on the card itself. Check the icons on the card to see what happens vs each opponent card type — the same icon system from the base game applies.
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes

Promo cards go in the Market deck, not in anyone's starting hand.
Shuffle all 4 promo cards into the Market deck during setup. They're purchased like any other Market card.

Chicken relics are cosmetic only.
They don't change any rules. Same cost, same purchase order. Use them if you like chickens.

The 6th player follows all normal rules.
No special rules for the extra player — same starting cards, same turnips, same win condition.

Seating matters more with 6.
You still only interact with immediate neighbors. With 6 players, the players across from you are completely out of reach. Think about seating balance.

Market deck is now 24 cards.
With 4 extra cards in the deck, the Market will last a bit longer. Still reveal only 4 face-up at a time.

Don't forget: The mandatory relic purchase rule applies to the Smuggler just like any other Merchant card. If you can afford your next relic when the Smuggler resolves, you must buy it.
