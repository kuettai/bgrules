💜 Village Pillage: Feelings

Mini-Expansion · Included in Big Box · Designer: Peter C. Hayward · Jellybean Games

"Now, for the first time ever, the ability to feel things."

📦 What's New

The Feelings expansion adds a small deck of Feeling cards — emotion-themed cards that give your villagers an emotional state each round, modifying how your cards resolve.

  • Feeling cards — a set of cards representing different emotions (e.g. Angry, Happy, Scared, Greedy, Sad) that attach to your played cards and alter their effects.
  • Emotional modifiers — each Feeling changes the outcome of a matchup in a small but meaningful way, adding a new layer of bluffing and mind-games.
💡 This is a lightweight mini-expansion — it adds flavor and variety without overhauling the core game. Great for mixing in once you're comfortable with the base game.
🔀 Rule Changes

How Feelings Work

Each round during the Plan phase, in addition to choosing which cards to play against each neighbor, you also assign a Feeling card to one (or both) of your played cards.

  1. During Plan, choose your 2 cards as normal, then attach an available Feeling card face-down to one of them.
  2. During Reveal, flip all cards and Feelings simultaneously.
  3. During Resolve, the Feeling modifies the attached card's effect for that matchup — the specific modification depends on which Feeling is attached.
  4. During Refresh, return Feeling cards to your available pool along with your played cards.

Feeling Effects

Each Feeling card changes the attached card's behavior. Consult the text printed on each Feeling card for its exact effect. Common patterns include:

😠 Angry — Boosts aggressive actions (Raiders steal more, Farmers become feisty).
😊 Happy — Boosts cooperative/gain actions (Farmers gain extra, Walls bank more).
😨 Scared — Defensive modifier (cards become more cautious — may reduce gains but protect from theft).
🤑 Greedy — Enhances Merchant effects or turnip acquisition at a cost.
😢 Sad — A wildcard that may reduce your own output but penalize your opponent too.
⚠️ Card-specific: Always read the Feeling card text. The effects above are general patterns — the exact interaction depends on which card the Feeling is attached to.
⚙️ Setup Changes
  1. Set up the base game as normal.
  2. Deal each player a set of Feeling cards (one of each emotion).
  3. Keep your Feeling cards face-up in front of you — they are open information.
💡 Feelings are a personal pool — each player has their own identical set. No shuffling into the market deck required.
⚠️ Gotchas / Common Mistakes
⚠️ Feelings are optional each round. You don't have to attach a Feeling — playing without one is a valid (and sometimes strategic) choice.
⚠️ Feelings modify, they don't replace. The base card still resolves its normal effect — the Feeling adds to or tweaks it. Don't skip the base card's resolution.
⚠️ Feelings are revealed simultaneously. Don't reveal your Feeling before the Reveal phase. They flip at the same time as all played cards.
⚠️ One Feeling per card, per round. You cannot stack multiple Feelings on the same card. You may attach one Feeling to one of your two played cards (or none at all).
⚠️ Resolve order unchanged. Feelings don't change the base game's resolve order (Farmer → Wall → Raider → Merchant). They only modify the effect within each step.
