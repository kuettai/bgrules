🎪 Village Pillage: Festivals

Mini-expansion · Included in the Big Box · Jellybean Games · "Festivals for everyone (but mostly for you)"

📦 What's New

The Festivals expansion adds a deck of Festival cards — shared events that affect all players at the table each round. Instead of a static game state, festivals introduce a rotating global modifier that changes the rules of engagement from turn to turn.

Festival cards create effects like:

  • Bonus turnip harvests — all Farmers gain extra turnips this round
  • Raider frenzies — Raiders steal more (or less) than usual
  • Market sales — reduced costs on Market card purchases
  • Defensive festivals — Walls gain extra banking or counter-steal bonuses
  • Chaos events — effects that shake up the normal resolve order or card interactions
The tagline says it all: "Festivals for everyone (but mostly for you)" — the key is reading which festival is active and choosing cards that exploit it better than your neighbors.

Each Festival card is a one-round global effect visible to all players before they choose their cards for the round. This adds a layer of tactical planning on top of the base game's bluffing.

🔀 Rule Changes

The core turn structure stays the same (Plan → Reveal → Resolve → Refresh), with one addition:

  1. Reveal Festival — at the start of each round, before the Plan phase, flip the top card of the Festival deck face-up. This is the active festival for the round.
  2. Plan — players choose their 2 cards as normal, but now with knowledge of the active festival's effect.
  3. Reveal & Resolve — resolve as normal, applying the festival's modifier to all affected card types.
  4. Discard Festival — after the round resolves, discard the festival card. Flip a new one next round.
Festivals affect ALL players equally. The festival effect applies to every matchup at the table, not just yours. Plan accordingly — if a festival boosts Farmers, expect your neighbors to Farm too.

When the Festival deck runs out: Shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck. Festivals cycle continuously until the game ends.

⚙️ Setup Changes

Set up the base game as normal, then:

  1. Shuffle the Festival deck and place it face-down in the center of the table near the Market.
  2. Leave space next to it for a Festival discard pile.

No changes to starting cards, turnips, relics, bank cards, or market setup.

Combines with other expansions. The Festival deck is a standalone module — it layers on top of any Village Pillage expansion combination (Surf & Turf, Robbin' Hood, Dark Arts & Crafts, etc.).
⚠️ Gotcha / Common Mistakes

Reveal the festival BEFORE choosing cards.
The whole point is that players see the festival and adapt. Don't flip it after cards are already played face-down.

Festivals apply to all matchups, not just yours.
If a festival says "Raiders steal +2," that applies to every Raider at every matchup around the table — including the ones raiding you.

Festival effects don't override card text.
If a festival boosts Raiders but your Wall still blocks them, the Wall still works. Festivals modify values, they don't remove card interactions.

Don't forget to discard the festival after the round.
Each festival lasts exactly one round. Flip a new one at the start of the next round.

Reading the festival is free information — use it! New players often ignore the active festival and play their "usual" strategy. The whole expansion rewards adapting your card picks to the current event.
